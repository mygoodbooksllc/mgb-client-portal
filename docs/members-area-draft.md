# Members area

HR › Members area is where you help the people who bought the course or the Launch Kit, and the clients who use the client library. You only see it if the owner has turned it on for you.

## What you see

A list of customers with their name, email, church, what they have access to, when they started, when they last signed in, and a summary of their purchases. Search by email, name or church. Use the access picker to show just Client, Course or Launch Kit. "Show test customers" is off by default so test accounts don't clutter the list.

Some people appear with "No sign-in account yet". They are on the client roster or have bought something, but they haven't signed in. You can see them, but there is nothing to open yet.

## Opening a customer

If the owner has given you permission, each customer with an account has an Open button. We ask why you are opening them first: Sign-in help, Purchase question, or Other (add a few words). Opening is recorded with your name, the customer, the time and your reason. Then you see their account, access, purchases (with receipt numbers) and support status.

If you don't have permission, the list is for looking only. Ask the owner if you need it.

## For the owner

The "Who can open customers" card lists every admin. Turn a person on to let them open customers. You always can.
