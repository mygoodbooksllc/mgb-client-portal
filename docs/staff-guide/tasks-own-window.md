Yes. Slate can sit in a slim window of its own, for example next to QuickBooks or on a second screen, while the portal stays open in your main window.

### Open it

On the Slate page (`#/tasks/my`), or on the **Slate** tab of the Desk, click **Open in own window** at the top right. A narrow window opens with just Slate: no rail and no top bar. It remembers its size next time.

The button is there on a computer with a window wider than about 760 pixels. It isn't on phones and tablets, or in a narrow window.

You can also type the portal's address with `/tasks` at the end (for example in a new tab or a bookmark). It opens the same slim view after you sign in. Today is still where the full portal starts.

### What's in the slim window

- A bar across the top with **Slate**, search, **Dock in portal** and your account menu, which has **Open full portal** and **Help**.
- All your lists: Slate, Search, Projects and Clients. In a narrow window they sit behind the **Lists** button. On a phone, a bar at the bottom has **Slate**, **Lists**, **Search** and **Add**.
- Task details, quick add (**q** works here too) and the keyboard shortcuts, the same as in the portal.

The slim window and the portal stay in sync: a change in one shows in the other within a moment. Opening a client from the slim window opens that client's pages with the full portal around them.

Tours and the first-30-days tips don't show in the slim window. Use the Slate page inside the portal for those.

### Follow my client

- The **Follow my client** switch is in the slim window's top bar. On a phone or tablet it's in the account menu.
- When it's on, opening a client in the portal switches the slim window to that client's list.
- Staff pages leave it on the last client.
- It's remembered in this browser, and both windows need to be open in the same browser.

### Dock it back

Click **Dock in portal**. If the portal window that opened it is still open, Slate opens there, on the same list, and the slim window closes. If that window is gone, the slim window turns into the full portal.

**View as** (admins) never opens the own-window Slate, and a new window opened during View as is your own normal app.

### Troubleshooting

- **"Your browser blocked the new window. Allow pop-ups for this site and try again."** Allow pop-ups for the portal's address in your browser's settings, then click **Open in own window** again.
- **I don't see Open in own window.** Widen the window, or use a computer. On a phone or tablet, open Slate from the top bar instead.
- **The slim window keeps opening slim after a refresh.** That's expected for that tab. Click **Dock in portal** or **Open full portal** to get the full portal back.
