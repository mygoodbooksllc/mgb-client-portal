The Pro tour is a short walkthrough that lives on a Basic client's **Plan** page, which is their sales page. It takes about two minutes and shows what Pro would add to the portal they already use. Nothing changes for the client unless they choose to ask for an upgrade, and the tour never starts on its own.

It's separate from the first-sign-in tour. See [How does the client guided tour work?](#/help/client-tour) for that one.

### Who gets it

- **Clients on Basic.** Any person at a Basic organization can take it, whether they have full or limited access. A client still on the retired Plus plan is shown as Basic, so they get it too.
- **Not clients on Pro.** There's nothing to sell them, so the button and the Pro tour row don't show.
- **Staff**, in two ways: previewing as a client user, or looking at the client's **Plan** page. See "How you preview it" below.

### Where a client starts it

It's always the client's choice. There are four places:

1. **Settings** > **Plan**: the **Plans** card at the top has a **Take the Pro tour** button.
2. **Home**: the locked card on a Basic client's Home (the one with **See plans**) also has a **Take the Pro tour** link. It opens **Settings** > **Plan** and starts the tour.
3. **Settings** > **Help**: a **Pro tour** row, "See what Pro adds, step by step". While you preview as a client user it reads **Preview the Pro tour**.
4. **The client tour's Plan stop** (for Basic clients): it ends by saying they can take the Pro tour there.

All four start the same tour. Every step opens on **Settings** > **Plan**, whichever page they were on.

### What it shows

The page dims and one part of the Plan page is highlighted at a time, with **Step 4 of 11**, **Back**, **Next** and **Skip tour**, just like the client tour. The Plan page is long, so each step scrolls its part into view, on a phone too.

1. **Welcome**: "See what Pro would do for" their organization, a two-minute look at what Pro adds. Nothing changes unless they ask for it.
2. **Plans**: the plan cards. Basic updates their books once a month, on the 15th. Pro keeps them live, synced from QuickBooks every 15 minutes, with a **Sync now** button, and adds the tools that follow. The price is on each plan card; the tour doesn't quote one.
3. **Board-ready Financial Overview**: **Home** becomes a fuller snapshot, with click-to-jump numbers, a low-cash alert and a one-click PDF.
4. **Report Builder**: a board packet in one PDF, with comparisons to budget and last year, a Statement of Functional Expenses and read-only links for the board. It lives on the **Reports** tab.
5. **Budgeting Tool**: year-end forecasts by category, what-if scenarios and next year's budget drafted together and approved by the board. It lives under **Finances** > **Budget**.
6. **Cash Flow Pro**: every bill in one place, batch pay runs with an approval step, duplicate-bill detection and an ACH export. It lives under **Finances** > **Cash flow**.
7. **Reconciliation Pro**: a real month-end close on **Bank accounts**, with every period signed off and kept.
8. **Fund Accounting Pro**: money moving between funds with a reason, pledges from committed to received, and year-end giving statements. It lives under **Finances** > **Giving & funds**.
9. **Pro, tool by tool**: the comparison list below the cards. "Every tool, line by line. Click one to see exactly what you get."
10. **Payroll**: only when the organization doesn't have the add-on. Payroll can be added on either plan, and **Add Payroll** asks us to set it up.
11. **Upgrade to Pro** (the last step, with a **Finish** button): when they're ready, the button sends a request to the bookkeeper, who switches the plan and confirms the price. The tour points at the button but never clicks it for them.

Each feature step says where the tool shows up in their portal, so they can match it to the tabs they already see. A step is left out when it doesn't apply, and the step count always matches what they'll see.

### Nothing changes until they ask

The tour is read-only. It doesn't switch anyone's plan, send anything, or add a charge. A plan only changes after the client clicks **Upgrade to Pro** themselves, and even then it's a request to us, not an automatic switch: the Plan page says "Upgrading isn't automatic yet."

### What happens after they click Upgrade to Pro

1. The request shows on **Today** in the **Now** band of **Needs you**, with the client's name, "Wants Pro (on Basic)" and who asked and when.
2. Follow up with the client, confirm the price, and switch the plan on the client's row in **Settings** › **Firm settings** › **Client roster**.
3. Mark the row **Contacted**, then **Completed** once the plan is switched, or **Dismiss** it.

Add Payroll requests arrive the same way. See [What's on my Today page?](#/help/home-page), [Account manager guide](#/help/account-manager-guide) (the Plans and upgrades section) and [Pricing milestones](#/help/pricing-milestones) for how fees follow the milestone.

### How you preview it

The tour never starts on its own for staff, and nothing is saved while you preview.

1. Open a Basic client and use **Preview as** to pick one of their people.
2. Click the **Settings** gear, then **Plan**, then **Take the Pro tour**. (Or **Help**, then **Preview the Pro tour**.)

You can also open **Settings** > **Plan** from the client's tab row without previewing as anyone, and start it from the **Plans** card there. If the client is already on Pro, **Preview plan** > **Basic** shows you the Basic version of the page first. See [How do I see the portal the way a client sees it?](#/help/preview-as).

### Where it's saved and counted

- When a real client person finishes or skips it, that's saved with their personal settings (`proTour`). Because it never starts by itself, this is just a record. It doesn't stop them taking the tour again whenever they like.
- Previews and staff looking at the Plan page aren't saved or counted.
- In **Settings** › **Firm settings** › **Usage stats**, under **Actions**, a finished Pro tour counts as one of **Tours finished** and a skipped one as one of **Tours skipped**. See [Usage stats and Developer tools](#/help/admin-insight).

### Troubleshooting

- **There's no Take the Pro tour button.** The client is on Pro. Check **Plan** on **Settings**, which marks **Your plan**. Under **Preview plan**, choose **Basic** to see the Basic version.
- **The Payroll step is missing.** The organization already has the Payroll add-on, so there's nothing to offer.
- **A client asks what Pro costs.** The price is on each plan card on their **Plan** page, on top of the milestone fee. Payroll pricing isn't shown there; they message their account manager for it.
- **A client says they clicked Upgrade to Pro and nothing happened.** Nothing switches by itself. Look for the request on **Today** under **Needs you**, then follow up with them.
