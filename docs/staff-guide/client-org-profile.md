A client's **organization profile** holds the few details we need to address things to them correctly and celebrate their anniversaries: the name for cards and gifts, the kind of organization, its **Founding date**, where gifts go, who receives deliveries, a main contact and the fiscal year end. Nothing in it emails anyone or orders anything.

### What clients see

- When a client signs in and their profile isn't finished, a short form opens: **Finish your organization profile**. It takes about two minutes. **Later** puts it off until their next browser session (a new tab or window), and they can save part of it and finish later.
- Until it's finished, their Home shows **Finish your organization profile** under Needs you, and their **?** menu has **Organization profile**.
- **Get to know you** questions (favorite treat, coffee or tea, allergies, hobbies, a note for the team) are optional. Once sent, the client never sees the answers again; sending new ones replaces them.
- Clients you are viewing as, previewing or impersonating never get the form.

### For everyone on staff

- A client's **Overview** has an **Organization profile** card under Key dates. The pill says **Not started**, **Incomplete** or **Complete**.
- If the client's fiscal year end differs from Key dates, the card says so. Key dates is never changed for you: check with the client and update Key dates yourself if needed.
- **Client since** comes from the client's launch date. Use **Edit** to set it by hand (for clients who started before the portal), and **Use launch date** to go back.
- **Get to know them** answers show only to admins, the client's assigned bookkeeper and the account manager. Everyone else sees that answers are in, not what they say.
- The client's onboarding checklist shows **Client finished organization profile**, ticked by itself when the profile is complete. You can't tick it by hand.

### People to contact

**Where:** a client's Overview › Organization profile › **People to contact**.

This is the one list of people we email for a client. The client keeps it up to date in their org profile, and you can add or fix people here.

- **Main contact.** Exactly one. Monthly reports go here. To change who it is, edit another person and tick **Main contact**. If you remove the main contact, the client is asked to pick a new one.
- **Monthly emails.** This person is preselected in the To line of monthly email drafts.
- **Can answer requests.** This person can be sent Ask client links. People the client adds start with this off unless the client ticks it.
- **Title** is what the client sees (for example Treasurer or Pastor). **Role** and **Notes** are for staff only; the client never sees them. Do not put private remarks in Title.
- **Added by client** marks people the client added. **Opted out** means they turned off portal emails; they are never preselected.
- Rows marked **Portal login** or **from org profile** are read-only here. Ask the client to update their org profile, or add the person here.

Changing someone's email, turning off Can answer requests, or removing them stops any open Ask client links sent to them.

**Greeting name.** What we call the main contact in emails ("Hi Pastor Jim"). If it differs from the Client info greeting, the card flags it.

**Where else this shows.** The Review board's contact list (the ▸ arrow) and the To chips in email drafts read this list, with where each person came from. The tasks drawer and Ask client use the same contacts.

View as never changes anything.

### For admins

**Team › Client gifts** (real admins only; not during View as) has:

- **Anniversary to follow.** One choice for the whole firm: **Founding date (default)**, the date the organization was founded, or **Client since**, the date they became a client. With Founding date, a client who answered **Not sure** or left the date blank still shows on their Client since date, so nobody drops off the list. Client since stays on file either way and shows next to the date in the list. The choice changes the list below and the weekly digest count, and every change is recorded with who made it.
- **Anniversaries in the next 30 days.** Each client's anniversary on the date chosen above (**Founded** or **Client since** in the Occasion column), with their **Client since** date and, when known, the founding date next to it. For each row: **Plan** (item, amount, notes; the amount starts at $40), **Mark sent** and **Skip**. The pill shows **Not planned**, **Planned**, **Sent** or **Skipped**. An amount outside the usual $30-45 budget gets a note but still saves.
- **Past gifts.** What was sent or skipped, with who marked it sent and when.
- **This week's digest line.** The weekly digest shows "Anniversaries coming up: N clients" while the firm switch is on.
- **Import dry run.** Paste the old anniversary sheet as CSV to see how many rows match a client, which don't, and which have bad dates, a missing city or ZIP, or a PO box. Nothing is saved: the real import waits for owner approval.

Nothing on this page emails clients or orders gifts. Buy and send the gift the usual way, then **Mark sent**.
