A month is finished in two steps: the bookkeeper marks it **Ready for sign-off**, then an admin **signs it off**. The sign-off is what makes the month **Done**.

### Bookkeepers: Ready for sign-off

When your close steps are ticked and the month is ready, click **Mark {Month} ready for sign-off** on the client's **Close** tab, or pick **Ready for sign-off** for the month on **Work › Close** and **Save**. The month shows as **Ready** in the grid. Nothing is emailed to the client yet.

You can't pick **Done** and you can't reopen a signed-off month. If you try another way, the app says *Only an admin sign-off can close a period.* or *A signed-off period can only be reopened by an admin, with a reason.* Ask an admin.

Your on-time score counts from when you marked the month ready, not from when the admin signed it off.

### Admins: Sign off

On the client's **Close** tab click **Sign off {Month}**, or pick **Done (sign off)** on **Work › Close** and click **Sign off**.

- **Signing off emails the client's people that the month's reports are ready.** If the same person already got that email for that month in the last 7 days, it isn't sent again.
- **Open tasks warn, they don't block.** If shared tasks linked to the month are still open, you see how many (and up to five names) and *Sign off anyway?*
- **Ledger review** still applies: if the review isn't approved, you're asked first (or blocked, if Team › Ledger review is set to Block).
- If someone else saved the month while you were looking at it, you see **Someone else saved first**. Load theirs, then sign off again.

The month then says *{Month} is signed off · {name} · {date}*.

### Reopening

Only an admin can reopen a signed-off month. Click **Reopen** on the client's Close tab (or pick another status on Work › Close and click **Reopen**) and say why, in at least 3 characters. The month goes back to **In progress**, the reason is kept with it, the ledger approval is cleared, and no email is sent. Signing it off again later works the same as the first time.

### Quarters and years

The database can also hold a sign-off for a calendar quarter or a fiscal year (from the client's fiscal year end). Quarter and year sign-offs never email the client. There's no screen for them yet; they come with recurring tasks.

Changing a client's fiscal year end is admin-only once a fiscal year has a sign-off record.
