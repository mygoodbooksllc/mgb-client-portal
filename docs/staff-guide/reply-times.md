**Reply times** shows how fast we answer client messages. Find it at **Team** → **Reply times** (or `#/team/reply-times`), admins only. Everyone sees the conversations waiting on them as the **Replies waiting** tile on [Today](#/help/home-page).

### How a reply is timed

- The clock starts at a client's **first** message in a conversation that's waiting on us. If they send three messages in a row, that's one wait, timed from the first.
- It stops at the next message a staff member sends in that conversation. That person gets the credit.
- **Internal** notes and deleted messages don't count.
- The goal is a reply within **24 hours**. These are **calendar hours**: nights, weekends and holidays all count. The goal is fixed in the app; ask the developer to change it.

### What's on the tab

1. Period tabs: **This month**, **Last month**, **Last 90 days**. A wait belongs to the day it started.
2. Four tiles: **Median reply** (half of replies are faster), **Slowest 10% (p90)**, **Within 24 h** (share of answered waits), and **Waiting over 24 h** (right now, whatever the period).
3. **Waiting for a reply now**: every conversation with no reply for more than 24 hours, oldest first. Click the client to open their messages.
4. **By staff member**: replies, median, p90 and share within 24 hours for each person.
5. **By client**: the same per client, slowest first, with how many are waiting now.

When there are no client messages yet, the tables say so.

### Who sees what

- Admins see everyone and every client.
- A temporary admin who opens this tab sees only their own replies and their own clients.
- Clients never see any of this.

### Replies waiting (Today tile)

Counts the client conversations waiting on a reply from us (your clients; every client for admins). Click it to open the Inbox. The median and over-24-hour numbers are on this tab, for admins.
