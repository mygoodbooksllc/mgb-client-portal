Welcome! The MyGoodBooks portal is where our clients (mostly churches and nonprofits) see their finances, and where we, the staff, keep track of our work for them. This page shows you around.

### Your first day

1. [Sign in](#/help/signing-in) with your @mygoodbooks.org Google account.
2. If you're a bookkeeper, the **Bookkeeper tour** starts by itself. It's about three minutes and walks through your day: Today, your clients, the Inbox, Work and Team. You can run it again any time from **?** → **Bookkeeper tour**. Admins get the **Admin tour** instead (the Bookkeeper tour is in their **?** menu), account managers also get an **Account manager tour**, and anyone given temporary admin access a **Temporary admin tour**, each once. See [Bookkeeper guide](#/help/bookkeeper-guide), [Admin guide](#/help/admin-guide), [Account manager guide](#/help/account-manager-guide) and [Temporary access](#/help/temporary-access).
3. You land on **Today**, one list of what needs you across all the clients you can see. See [What's on my Today page?](#/help/home-page).
4. Open a client from **Clients**, or press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) and type their name. You'll see that client's **Client overview** first. See [How do I find and open a client?](#/help/finding-a-client).
5. Your new-hire checklist is on **Team › Onboarding**. See [How does the new-hire onboarding checklist work?](#/help/new-hire-onboarding).
6. Stuck? Click **?** in the top bar, then **Help for this page**, or search this guide.

### The five places

The dark rail on the left has five places. Everything you do lives in one of them:

- **Today**: what needs you, most urgent first. See [What's on my Today page?](#/help/home-page).
- **Inbox**: client conversations and team chat in one place. See [How do I message a client or a teammate?](#/help/inbox).
- **Work**: your **Tasks**, the month-end **Close** and filing **Deadlines**. See [What's on the Work page?](#/help/work-page).
- **Clients**: every client you can see, and the way into a client's pages. See [How do I use the Clients page?](#/help/clients-page).
- **Team**: **People** (who's out, your backups, time off, shout-outs), **Reviews** and **Onboarding**. Admins also see **Hours**, **Reply times**, **Feedback** and **Members**. See [What's on the Team page?](#/help/team-page).

At the bottom of the rail: **Settings** (your profile, notifications, theme, start page and signature; admins also find the firm-wide pages there) and **Collapse**, which shrinks the rail to icons. See [How do I change my settings?](#/help/staff-settings).

Every page looks the same: a title, one row of tabs, and the content. The tab is part of the address, so you can copy a link to exactly where you are.

### The top bar

Across the top: the client picker, the sync pill (client pages), search (**Ctrl+K** / **⌘K**, which also jumps to any page or action), the bell, **Tasks**, **+** (add a task, note or document request, or message the client), **?** (help for this page, the staff guide, **Send feedback**, **What's new** and the tours) and your initials (dark mode, Preview as and **Sign out**). See [What's on the bar at the top of the page?](#/help/top-bar).

### Inside a client

Open a client and the title becomes **Clients › {client} › {tab}**, with one row of tabs: **Overview**, the client's own pages (Dashboard, Budget, Bank, Documents, Messages and so on), **SOP** and **Milestone**, with **Settings** at the right end. **View as client** shows the pages exactly as the client sees them. Click **Clients** in the breadcrumb, or the browser's **Back** button, to return to the list.

### On a phone

The top bar keeps the client picker, search, the bell and **+**. Tap the menu button at the top left to open the five places, **Settings** and **Sign out**. Inside a client, the tab row scrolls sideways.

### Who sees what

- **Bookkeepers** see only the clients they're assigned to, and the Team tabs **People**, **Reviews**, **Onboarding**, **Performance** and **Inventory**. See [Bookkeeper guide](#/help/bookkeeper-guide).
- **Admins** see every client, every Team tab, and the firm-wide pages under **Settings**.
- Clients never see staff notes, SOPs, internal notes or anything on the staff pages.

### Good to know

- Links work: copy the address of a page (for example a client's Documents, or **Work › Close**) and paste it into chat. The other person lands on the same page after signing in, as long as they're allowed to see it.
- Refreshing the page keeps you where you were.
- Test clients show sample data where QuickBooks has nothing yet (for example funds and payroll), with a sample-data banner. Real clients never see sample numbers, even if their id matches a sample client: those pages show a short "appears here once…" note instead. Giving and funds come from QuickBooks.
