Offboarding hands a departing staff member's clients and open tasks to someone else and deactivates them, all in one step. Their staff record and history stay.

### Steps

1. Go to **Team → Members** and click **Offboard** on their row. (You can't offboard yourself.)
2. **Replacements**: you'll see how many clients, open tasks and access grants they have.
   - **One person takes everything**: pick a **Replacement**.
   - **Choose per client**: pick a default replacement (also gets tasks with no client), then change individual clients (**Same as above** or **No one (just unassign)**).
   - With no replacement, clients are just unassigned and open tasks come to you.
3. Click **Review**. Check the list of what will happen: clients moved, open tasks moved, live grants revoked, pending requests cancelled, temporary admin access ended, and the person deactivated. Click **Continue**.
4. **Confirm**: type their name and click **Offboard {name}**.

The done screen lists the results. Everything is recorded in the Audit log.

### What isn't touched

Finished tasks, time, messages and audit history stay as they are.

### Troubleshooting

- **I need them back.** Reactivate them on Team → Members (tick **Active**). Their clients and tasks won't move back on their own; reassign them with **Manage**.
