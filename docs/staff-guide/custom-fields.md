Part of [Slate plus](#/help/slate-plus). A project or a client's task list can carry up to 15 extra fields, such as **Fund**, **Grant** or **Board meeting date**, so every task in it records the same facts.

### See and set up fields

Open the project or client list and click **Fields** in its header (it shows a count when there are any). Everyone who can see the list can read its fields.

- **Who changes them.** On a project, admins and the project's owner. On a client's list, admins only. Everyone else sees a note saying so.
- **Add a field.** Give it a name (up to 40 characters, not the same as another field in that list) and a type: **Text**, **Number**, **Dropdown**, **Date**, **Checkbox** or **Person**. A Dropdown needs 1 to 20 options. The type can't change later; make a new field instead.
- **Rename, reorder and edit options** from the same panel. Renaming a dropdown option renames it on every task.
- **Archive** hides a field everywhere but keeps every value. **Show archived** lists them with **Restore**.
- **Remove a dropdown option** that tasks still use and those tasks keep it, marked **(retired)**; it just can't be picked again.
- At 15 fields, archive one to add another.

### Fill them in

Open a task. Its fields are in the details, under **Blocked by**. A task in a project tied to a client shows the project's fields first, then the client's.

- Text and Number save when you click away or press **Enter** (**Esc** undoes your typing).
- Dropdown, Date, Checkbox and Person save as soon as you pick.
- Anyone who can edit the task can fill its fields. Followers and read-only views see them grayed out.
- Hover a field's label to see who set it last, and when.

### On the task row

Filled fields show as small chips on the row, such as **Fund: Building** (a ticked Checkbox shows just its name). You see three at most, then **+N**; hover **+N** for the rest. Empty fields and archived fields show no chip.

### Good to know

- Move a task to another project or client and its old values are kept but hidden, because only the new list's fields apply. Move it back and they show again.
- In the narrow Slate app window the Fields panel is read-only. Make the window wider to edit.
- Fields are staff only. Clients never see them, and they're not in **View as**.
