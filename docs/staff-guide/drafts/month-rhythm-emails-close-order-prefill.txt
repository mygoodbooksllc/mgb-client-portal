### Draft email (Check in, Send reports, Follow up)

Three month rhythm tasks come with a ready-made client email:

- **Request for information** on the second Check in task: what the client still owes us for the month (open Ask client requests, uncategorized bank items, open document requests).
- **Reports email** on the second Send reports task: the month's reports, with a Preliminary note listing what is still missing when the reports went out early.
- **Mid-month update** on the first Follow up task: where the month stands and what is still open.

Open the task and choose **Draft email: …**, or click the envelope next to the task's name in a list. Slate builds the email from the client's latest data, signed with your name. Edit the subject and body if you like, then choose **Copy email** (or **Copy subject**) and paste it into your own email. **Slate never sends anything.** **Start over** rebuilds the draft from the data.

If some data could not be read (for example QuickBooks is not connected), the draft says so under the body so you can fill that part in yourself.

**Emails due** on your Slate shows only your open email tasks that are due today or earlier.

### Month view: The thirds or Close order (a client's task list)

A client's task list has a **Month view** choice:

- **The thirds** (the default): the month's tasks under Check in, Send reports and Follow up, as before.
- **Close order**: each client month as one group ("September 2026 close"), its steps in the order you work them. A green **Next** pill marks the first open step; **Waiting on client** marks a step that is waiting or has an open Ask client request. Due dates show as a target and are never red.

The choice is yours for that client, in this browser. Nobody else's view changes and no task changes. Close order steps aside while you sort by a column, use Board, or group by client, and it turns off manual order for that list.

### Pre-fill Quarterly and Annual work by client type (admins)

On **Slate › Recurring**, admins see **Pre-fill Quarterly and Annual work by client type**. Pick Churches, Other nonprofits or For-profit businesses, tick the suggestions you want, and choose **Make N drafts**.

- Each suggestion becomes a **draft** template for every real client of that type (test clients and the firm's own record are left out). A draft never makes tasks until you open it, check its clients and dates, and activate it.
- A suggestion that already exists for those clients shows as **already a template** and is not made again.
- Filings (990, 1099, W-2, 941, 940) are not suggested. They keep their one date on **Work › Deadlines**.
- Occasional work (a new pastor, a new grant, a new loan) is listed as ideas only. Add it as a one-off task when it comes up.

While the month rhythm is on, a template task for a quarter or a year that has no section shows under **Quarterly** or **Annual** on the client's list.
