At launch, paste the entry below at the top of the entries in `docs/staff-guide/whats-new.md` (after the intro lines), re-dated to launch day, and add these keywords to its front matter: `review board, work review board, deadline digest, digest in the bell, 4th 14th 24th, check in, send reports, follow up, open in email app, mailto, mark sent, to and cc, copy draft`. Then sync the guide row.

### <launch day>: Review board (admins)
- **Work › Review board** shows every client's **Check in** (4th), **Send reports** (14th) and **Follow up** (24th) tasks for a month on one screen, with each client's bookkeeper, backup, main contact and close status. A chip says who did each one and when, or who it is waiting on; red means late. Click a chip to open the task.
- **Every column sorts**, including **Bookkeeper**. Filter by bookkeeper or by status (Has late, Any open, All three done, No task yet, Close not done, Preliminary reports). The ▸ arrow shows a client's contacts and portal logins.
- **Deadline digest in the bell** (for admins on the owner's digest list): one entry per deadline each morning, like **41 of 62 done, 21 open**, which opens the board on that month.
- **Email drafts** on month-rhythm tasks now have **To** and **Cc** chips, **Copy draft**, **Open in email app** and **Mark sent** (who and when, with Undo). Nothing is sent from the portal; you still send from your own email.
