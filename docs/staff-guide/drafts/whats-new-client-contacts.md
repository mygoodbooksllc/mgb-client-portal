**People to contact, in one place.** Each client's Organization profile now has a People to contact list: the main contact (monthly reports go here), who gets monthly emails, and who can answer our questions. Clients keep it up to date in their org profile and can add the other people we should contact; you can add, edit or remove people on the client's Overview. The Review board and email drafts now use this list, so the To line starts with the right people. Staff notes and role stay staff-only.
