### 2026-10-21: Waiting on a client is never late

A task that is waiting on a client no longer counts as late anywhere: not on Today, the Team page, Slate's Overdue group, on-time stats or the performance score. It shows in the **Waiting** group instead. The same goes for monthly tasks in a month whose reports went out preliminary. See [How do I use my Slate?](#/help/my-tasks).

--- task-review / my-tasks paragraph ---

**Waiting is never late.** While a task waits on the client (Ask client), it does not count as late, and it is left out of on-time stats and the performance score. Monthly tasks for a month whose reports went out preliminary are treated the same way. Once the client answers and the task is open again, the normal due date applies.
