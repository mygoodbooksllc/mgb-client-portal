# Client help: org profile contacts (DRAFT, held)

Client-facing ? menu and help text for the org profile's new fields. Held: the live client org-profile help and tour stay unchanged until the owner turns on `org_contacts_enabled`. Test clients (`test_only`) see the form already.

## Who should we contact?

**Main contact.** The person we send your monthly reports to. An email is required.

**What should we call them?** Optional. The name we use in emails, for example "Pastor Jim".

**Other people we should contact.** Add anyone else we should keep in the loop, up to 24 people:
- **Gets monthly emails**: they get the monthly reports email too.
- **Can answer our questions**: we may send them a secure link when we need a document or an answer.

You can edit or remove anyone on the list, including people our team added. Removing someone, or changing their email, stops any open links we sent them.
