A client's monthly fee follows their **confirmed pricing milestone**. The milestone is suggested from their numbers (monthly transactions and annual expenses), but a person confirms it.

### The milestones

| | Name | Fee / month |
|---|---|---|
| I | Starter | $250 |
| II | Foundation | $300 |
| III | Growth | $400 |
| IV | Expanding | $600 |
| V | Established | $700 |
| VI | Advanced | $800 |
| VII | Strategic | $1,500 |
| VIII | Premier | $2,500 |
| IX | Enterprise | Custom |
| CP | Church Plant | $100 until launch |

### Where to see it

- For clients: the **Milestone** pill at the top of their sidebar, under the "Do good work. Leave the bookkeeping to us." tagline (click it to open the Milestone page). It's hidden when the sidebar is collapsed and for people with limited access. Staff open the client's **Milestone** tab instead.
- On the client's **Milestone** tab, under **Staff: budget and milestone**, where you can **Set milestone** if it isn't confirmed yet.
- **Today → Milestones to review** (turn it on under **Also show**): clients whose numbers point to a different milestone, aren't confirmed yet, or are close to the next one.
- The Monday **weekly digest**, in **Fee changes to review**: the same suggestions, emailed to admins. They're never applied automatically; a fee only changes when someone sets the milestone below.

### Set or change a milestone

Talk it through with the client first; their fee changes with it.

1. Open the client's Milestone page (or **Client details → Milestone**). The staff section is at the top.
2. Optional: under **Annual expenses**, enter an **Amount**, **Source** (Form 990, Approved budget, Other) and **As of** date, then **Save budget**. Without it, QuickBooks numbers are used.
3. Under **Set milestone**, choose the **Milestone** (the suggested one is tagged "suggested"), add a **Note (optional)** such as *Discussed on the Oct 3 call*, and click **Set milestone**.

**History** lists every change, who made it and the numbers at the time (including moves off Church Plant, shown as *Church Plant → …*).

### Reading the Milestone page

- **Updated …** is the client's last QuickBooks sync, the same time as the sync pill.
- The second meter says **Annual budget (expenses)** when the number is an entered budget (Form 990, Approved budget, Other) or the QuickBooks budget, and **Annual expenses** when it falls back to the last 12 months of actual expenses. The **Source** line under it names which one.
- A client on **Church Plant** stays there until you set another milestone; the numbers never move them off it.

### Troubleshooting

- **"The milestone appears once QuickBooks is connected."** Connect the client's QuickBooks first, or enter annual expenses by hand.
- **"Milestones turn on once the client-milestones database update is applied."** Tell the owner.
