Part of [Slate plus](#/help/slate-plus). Everyone on staff gets these; clients never see them, and they're not in **View as**.

### One search for everything

The search box in the top bar finds clients, tasks, projects, people, SOPs and help in one place. Press **Ctrl+K** (Windows) or **⌘K** (Mac) to jump to it from anywhere. Use the arrow keys and **Enter** to open a result, or **Esc** to close. On a phone, tap the search icon and the search opens across the top of the screen.

On the Slate page, the first result is **Filter this Slate list for "…"**. It narrows your Slate to your words instead of opening a result. A **Text** chip shows in the filter bar; click its **×** to clear it. This takes the place of the old search box next to **Add task**.

### Names open profiles

Click a person's name on a task row, in a task, in a comment, on **Team** or on a shout-out to open their profile. To change who a task is assigned to, use the small **Reassign** button (two arrows) next to their badge on the row.

A profile shows:

- **Who they are**: name, role, photo, their status and local time, and whether they're out of office (with who covers their clients).
- **This week at a glance**: open tasks, due this week, late and waiting on a client. Late is just a count here; it doesn't change anyone's score.
- **Their tasks**, grouped Late, This week, Later and Waiting on client. You can only change a task here if you could already change it elsewhere in Slate.
- **Clients they cover**, **Works most with**, **Recent work** and their latest **Shout-outs**.
- **Manager and team**: who they report to, their direct reports and the teams they're on.

You only see what someone has shared with **All staff**, and only tasks and clients you could already see somewhere else. Private tasks stay private. Hours aren't shown. You can't change anything on someone else's profile.

Admins also see the person's job title and when they were last seen, plus **Open full list** for their whole task list.

### Make your profile yours

Open your own profile (click your name on any task row, or search for yourself) to add:

- **Status**: a short line about what you're working on (up to 140 characters).
- **Local time**: pick your time zone so teammates see your local time. Until you pick one, your profile shows firm time (Central).
- **Pinned**: up to 12 clients and 12 projects you open most. They come first in Slate's sidebar panels.
- **Focus this quarter**: up to three goals. Tick one off when it's done. At the start of a new quarter, **Start this quarter's goals** starts fresh.
- **About me**: a few lines about you, your skills and your working hours.
- **Favorites**: client SOPs and links you use a lot (links must start with https://).
- **Out of office**: add time off and choose a backup for each of your clients, or the same backup for all of them. See [How do I add time off and make sure my clients are covered?](#/help/time-off-coverage).
- **Default view**: one of your saved filters to open Slate with. Only you see this.

Each section you can share has a **Visible to** choice: **Only me** or **All staff**. Status, About me and local time start as All staff. Pinned, Focus and Favorites start as Only me.

**Customize** lets you choose the order of the sections and hide the ones you don't use. The up and down arrows move a section (they work with the keyboard too), and **Reset to default** starts over. Everyone who opens your profile sees the same order.

There's no photo upload here. Your profile picture is the same one as everywhere else in the app (see [How do I add my profile photo?](#/help/profile-photo)).

### Team load

**Team › People** shows **Team load** at the top: for each person, open tasks, due this week and late, plus an **Unassigned** chip (open tasks nobody is on yet; it links to Slate's Unassigned group) and bars for the next 4 weeks. Click a name to open that person's profile.

- Admins and account managers see everyone. Everyone else sees their own row. Hours show for admins only.
- On a profile, admins and account managers see the same **Team load**, with that person highlighted. Your own profile has a **Your load** card.
- Team load used to sit at the top of the Slate page and in its Unassigned group. It's no longer there.

### For admins: managers and teams

- **Managers** come from **HR › Employee records** (the **Manager** field). A profile shows **Change in HR › Employee records** to admins.
- **Teams**: a **Teams** card on **Team › People**, under Team load. **New team** makes one (a name, an optional description and a color); then **Add people**. **Rename** and **Archive** are on each team. Archiving takes the team off everyone's profile and keeps its members on record.
- On someone else's **Out of office** card, admins also see **Manage in Team › Who's out**.

### Troubleshooting

- **A name doesn't open a profile.** Names stay plain in **View as**. Reload the page if Slate plus was only just turned on.
- **I don't see Team load.** It's on **Team › People**, not the Slate page. If it says only your own row, that's expected unless you're an admin or account manager.
- **My time zone shows Central.** You haven't picked one yet. Open your profile and set **Local time**.
