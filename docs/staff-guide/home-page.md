**Today** is your starting point: the one thing to do next, and a few small cards about everything around it. It's the first of the five places in the left rail (**Today**, **Inbox**, **Work**, **Clients**, **Team**), and it opens when you sign in unless you picked another start page in [Settings](#/help/staff-settings).

Today no longer repeats your whole list. The full list lives in **Work › Slate** and, once the owner turns it on, in the Desk on the right of every page (press **D**). See [How do I use the Desk?](#/help/workbench).

### The top of the page

A greeting, the date, and one line: **3 due today · 1 late · Open Slate**. **Due today** counts your own tasks due today, and **late** your own overdue tasks, filing deadlines and bills (with the Desk on too; the Desk's bar has its own counts). **Open Slate** goes to **Work › Slate**.

### Do next

One card with your top item: what it is, the client, when it's due, and **then:** the item after it. The button does the next thing (**Start** or **Open** on a task, **Reply** on a client message, **Sign**, **Open**...). A teammate asking for temporary access shows **Review**: it opens that request under **Also needs you**, where **Approve** and **Deny** sit side by side. Under the card, **N more tasks in Slate** links to the rest of your tasks (with the Desk on: **N more in your Desk**, which opens it, and a **Slate** link). The order is the same ranking your list has always used, most urgent first (late or waiting on us, then coming up). Admins who picked **Everyone** in the Desk still get their own top item here. When nothing is waiting, the card says **Clean slate. Nothing needs you right now.**

Items that need a decision, such as access and upgrade requests, handbook signatures, deadlines, bills, closes, client health, reviews and SOPs, sit under **Also needs you (N)** below the card (birthdays and anniversaries too, while the **This week** card is off). Click it to open them, with their **Approve**, **Contacted**, **Sign** and other buttons right on the row. Messages and tasks are not repeated there.

### The cards

Under Do next, a row of small cards that fits your window (side by side on a computer, stacked on a phone):

- **Clients needing attention**: up to four clients, each with why: **Reply waiting**, **Close blocked**, **At risk** or **Watch** (client health), or **No backup while out** (admins). Click one to go to the first reason: the conversation, the Close tab, or the health reasons on the client's Overview.
- **Waiting on others**: teammates asking you for temporary access (click to open the request under **Also needs you**), client portal access requests, upgrade and Add Payroll requests, and a count of client replies waiting on us. Click a line to go to it. Clients on Basic can look over what Pro adds first with the Pro tour; see [How does the Pro tour work?](#/help/pro-tour).
- **Your reminders**: a quick list with a box to add one. For times, repeats and sharing, open the reminder from **Work › Slate**.
- **This week**: birthdays and work anniversaries coming up (everyone gets a 7-day heads-up with a **Give a shout out** button; admins see the next 30 days with the date and days away, so there is time to send a gift, with **Gift sent** and **Dismiss** under each line), and **Milestones to review**: clients whose numbers point to a different pricing milestone, aren't confirmed yet, or are close to the next one.

Below them, **Recent clients** is a single line with the clients you opened lately on this device. Click one to go back. With the left sidebar on, those clients are listed in the sidebar instead and this card is hidden.

### Also show

At the bottom, **Also show** has a switch for each card: **Clients needing attention**, **Waiting on others**, **Reminders**, **This week** and **Recent clients**. All are on to start. Your choice is remembered.

### Looking for something that used to be on Today?

- The four number tiles and the long **Needs you** list are gone. Replies, tasks, reviews and files are in **Work › Slate**, the **Inbox**, and the Desk. Closes open: **Work › Close**.
- Your client list, with health, backup and hours: the **Clients** page. See [How do I use the Clients page?](#/help/clients-page).
- Month-end close progress and filing deadlines: **Work › Close** and **Work › Deadlines**. See [What's on the Work page?](#/help/work-page).
- Shout-outs, your time off and who's out: **Team › People**. Your onboarding checklist: **HR › Onboarding**. See [What's on the Team page?](#/help/team-page).
- Custom cards and **Customize dashboard** are not on Today. A client's Dashboard still has **Customize dashboard**.

In **View as** (admins), Today is always the classic page, read-only, and a line under the bar says the person's Desk is private to them.

### Troubleshooting

- **Do next is empty, but I know something's due.** Today only covers clients you can open. If one is missing, ask an admin to assign you from that client's settings.
- **I miss the long list.** Open **Work › Slate**, or press **D** for the Desk. Approvals and other less common items are under **Also needs you** on Today.
- **I'd rather have the old Today.** Click **?** on the Do next card and pick **Use classic Today in this browser**.
