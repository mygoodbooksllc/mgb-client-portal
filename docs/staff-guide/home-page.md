**Today** is your starting point: one list of everything waiting on you across every client you can see, most urgent first. It's the first of the five places in the left rail (**Today**, **Inbox**, **Work**, **Clients**, **Team**), and it opens when you sign in unless you picked another start page in [Settings](#/help/staff-settings).

### The top of the page

A greeting, a line such as **3 need you now**, and four tiles:

- **Replies waiting**: client conversations with no reply from us yet. Opens the **Inbox**.
- **Tasks due today** and **Overdue**: your own tasks. Open **Work › Tasks**.
- **Closes open**: your clients' month-end closes that aren't finished. Opens **Work › Close**.

A tile shows a green **0** when there's nothing to do. Click any tile to go to what it counts.

### Needs you (the list)

Everything waiting on you, in two bands: **Now** (late, or waiting on us) and **This week** (coming up, or worth a look). Each row says what it is and which client, and the most urgent rows come first. Click a row to go straight to it. Some rows have their buttons right there:

- **A client message** with no reply: opens the conversation in the Inbox.
- **A task or reminder** that's due: **Done** checks it off.
- **Bills** overdue or due within 7 days, one row per client with the count and total.
- **A month-end close** that's late or blocked: opens that month in **Work › Close**.
- **A filing deadline** coming up: opens **Work › Deadlines**.
- **A portal access request**, someone a client asked us to give access to: **Review** opens that client's Manage access panel.
- **A teammate asking for temporary access** to one of your clients: **Approve** or **Deny**.
- **An upgrade or Add Payroll request** from a client's Plan tab: follow up with them, then **Contacted**, **Completed** or **Dismiss**. Clients on Basic can look over what Pro adds first with the Pro tour; see [How does the Pro tour work?](#/help/pro-tour).
- **A client at risk**: health is **At risk** or **Watch**, with the score. Opens the client. See [What does client health mean?](#/help/client-health).
- **A stale SOP**: a client's SOP hasn't been edited or marked accurate in over 180 days. Open it, read it, then fix it or click **Mark as still accurate**.
- **A client you're covering** while their bookkeeper is out.
- **Your quarterly review** when it's due: **Open**. See [How do quarterly reviews work?](#/help/quarterly-reviews).

Each band shows its first few rows. **Show everything** lists the rest, and **Show less** folds it back. When nothing is waiting, the list says **Nothing needs you right now.**

### Also show

Under the list, **Also show** has three switches for cards you may want on Today. Your choice is remembered.

- **Reminders**: the **Your reminders** card, a quick list with a box to add one. For times, repeats and sharing, open **Work › Tasks**.
- **Milestones**: the **Milestones to review** card: clients whose numbers point to a different pricing milestone, aren't confirmed yet, or are close to the next one.
- **Recently viewed**: the clients you opened lately. Click one to go back.

### Looking for something that used to be on Home?

- Your client list, with health, backup and hours: the **Clients** page. See [How do I use the Clients page?](#/help/clients-page).
- Month-end close progress and filing deadlines: **Work › Close** and **Work › Deadlines**. See [What's on the Work page?](#/help/work-page).
- Shout-outs, your time off and who's out: **Team › People**. Your onboarding checklist: **Team › Onboarding**. See [What's on the Team page?](#/help/team-page).
- Custom cards and **Customize dashboard** are gone from Today. It's the same list for everyone, so things are always in the same place. A client's Dashboard still has **Customize dashboard**.

### Troubleshooting

- **The list is empty, but I know something's due.** Today only covers clients you can open. If one is missing, ask an admin to assign you from that client's settings.
- **The reply count looks off.** Some test clients still have sample messages. The Inbox has the real conversations.
