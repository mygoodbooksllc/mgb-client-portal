**Clients** (in the left rail) lists every client you can see, one per row, and is the way into a client's pages.

### The list

- **Mine** / **All** / **Needs attention** at the top filter the list. Bookkeepers start on **Mine**. **Needs attention** means health at risk, a late close, a portal access request waiting, or a stale SOP.
- **Find a client** narrows the list as you type.
- Columns: **Client**, **Health** (the score and band, see [What does client health mean?](#/help/client-health)), **Assigned to**, **Backup**, **Hours this month**, **Close** (last month's status) and **Last viewed**.
- **Hours this month** is QuickBooks Time this calendar month against the client's monthly hours budget, for example **6.5 / 10 h**. It turns amber from 80% and red when over. Everyone assigned to a client sees it, so you always know where you stand. A dash means no budget is set.

Click a row to open the client. You land on the [Client overview](#/help/client-overview).

### Inside a client

- The page title becomes a breadcrumb: **Clients › {client} › {tab}**. Click **Clients** to go back to the list. The browser's **Back** button does the same.
- One row of tabs: **Overview**, then the client's own pages (**Dashboard**, **Budget**, **Bank**, **Documents**, **Messages** and so on, depending on their plan), then **SOP** (staff only) and **Milestone**. The tab is part of the address, so you can send a teammate a link to, say, a client's Documents.
- **Settings** at the right end of the tab row opens [the client's Settings](#/help/client-settings): Manage access, Client details and the Plan. It shows **new access request pending** when someone is waiting.
- **View as client** shows the client's pages exactly as they see them, with the staff tools hidden. See [How do I see the portal the way a client sees it?](#/help/preview-as).

### Other ways to open a client

- Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) and type the client's name.
- Click the client picker at the top left of the page.
- On Today, click any row or the **Recently viewed** card.

### Troubleshooting

- **"No clients are assigned to you yet."** An admin can assign you from the client's settings (**Client details**), or from **Team › Members**.
- **Hours this month is blank.** QuickBooks Time isn't connected, the client's QuickBooks customer isn't mapped yet, or no budget is set. Admins fix mapping under **Team › Hours**.
