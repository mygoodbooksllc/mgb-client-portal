As an admin you see everything a bookkeeper sees, plus the pages that run the firm: people, hours and money, and the firm's settings. This is the written version of the **Admin tour**, with a routine to follow.

### Take the tour

- It starts by itself once, the first time you open the portal after you've seen the staff tour.
- Run it again any time from **?** → **Admin tour**, from **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) by typing "admin tour", or from **Settings › Help**.
- It opens each admin page in turn and points at it: **Team › Members**, **Hours**, **Reply times**, **Performance**, **Reviews**, **Feedback** and **Inventory**, then **Settings › Firm settings** and **Today**. About two minutes.

If you're also a client's account manager, the **Account manager tour** comes next. See [Account manager guide](#/help/account-manager-guide).

### A routine

**Every day**

1. Work through **Today**: client messages, access and upgrade requests, and clients at risk.
2. Glance at admin@mygoodbooks.org for any **"MyGoodBooks alert:"** email and deal with it first. See [What are the health-check alert emails?](#/help/health-alerts).

**Every week**

1. Read the weekly digest email.
2. **Team › Reply times**: is anyone falling behind the 24-hour goal?
3. **Team › Hours**: anyone over capacity, or a client over its hours budget?
4. **Team › Feedback**: sort new reports.
5. **Team › Inventory › Requests**: approve or order what's been asked for.

**Every month**

1. **Work › Close**: is every client's month closed? See [Month-end close](#/help/month-end-close).
2. On **Today**, turn on **Also show › Milestones** and confirm pricing milestones. See [Pricing milestones](#/help/pricing-milestones).
3. **Team › Hours**: check margin by client. See [Profitability](#/help/profitability).
4. **Settings › Usage stats**: what's used and who's gone quiet.

**Every quarter**

1. Open the review cycle on **Team › Reviews › Team status**. See [How do I run a review cycle?](#/help/review-cycles).

**When it comes up**

- A new client: [New client, start to finish](#/help/new-client-start-to-finish).
- A new hire: [Staff and access](#/help/staff-management) and [New-hire onboarding](#/help/new-hire-onboarding).
- Someone leaving: [Offboarding](#/help/offboarding).

### Good to know

- Every **View as** is recorded in the Audit log, and your own Settings are read-only while you're viewing as someone.
- Staff with temporary admin access see some of these pages but not all. See [Temporary access](#/help/temporary-access).
