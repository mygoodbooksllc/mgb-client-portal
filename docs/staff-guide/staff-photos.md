Every active staff member adds a profile photo. You tidy the background so the whole team matches. It all happens on **Team › Members** (`#/team/members`).

### The Photo column

Each person has a photo and a status:

- **Missing**: they haven't added a photo yet. They see a full-page prompt until they do.
- **Needs edit**: they added their own photo and nobody has edited it yet.
- **Done**: an admin's edited photo is the current one.
- **Not required**: marked by an admin, so they never see the prompt.

When people have photos waiting, **Today** shows **N profile photos to edit**. It comes back whenever someone uploads a new photo. Click it to open Team › Members at the first one.

### Edit one photo

Click a photo to open its **Profile photo** window.

1. Click **Download original**.
2. Remove the background in your editor. Export a **square PNG with a transparent background**, **512 px or larger**.
3. Click **Replace with edited photo**, pick the file, add a note if you like, and save. If the picture has no transparent background, you'll be asked to confirm.

The status becomes **Done**. If the person later uploads a new photo, it becomes **Needs edit** again and your edit stays in the history.

### Other actions

- **Download current** saves the photo everyone sees now.
- **Restore original** shows the person's own cropped photo again, after you confirm. Your edit stays in the history.
- **Not required**: for the rare person who can't have a photo. Available while they have none. Add a reason if you like. Switch it off to require a photo again.
- **History** lists every version with who added it and when. **Download** any version. **Hide** removes a version from the list (it's kept for the record, and you can't hide the current one or the only original).

### Download all

The **Profile photos** card above the roster has a **Download all (zip)** button. Pick **Current**, **Originals** or **Needs edit only (originals)**. It saves one zip, `mgb-staff-photos-<date>.zip`. Anything that couldn't be downloaded is listed afterward.

### Who can do what

Only admins can download originals or change photos. A bookkeeper with temporary admin access sees the Photo column but can't download or change anything. Nobody's stored photos are ever deleted.

### What gets logged

The [Audit log](#/help/audit-log) records photo submitted, replaced, restored, marked not required (and required again), version hidden and downloaded. Filter by the **Profile photos** action group.
