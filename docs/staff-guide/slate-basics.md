Slate shows every task on one list, with three controls, a labeled sidebar and an add line at the top.

### The sidebar
The left sidebar lists **My tasks**, **Waiting on client**, **To review**, **Clients**, **Projects** and **People**. Admins also see **Recurring**. In the Slate app at full size (and on the portal Slate page in a wide window) each item shows a name; in a narrow window only the icons show, with a short caption.

In the Slate app, the **account avatar** sits at the bottom of the sidebar. Click the avatar for My profile, Notifications, Display settings, Dark mode and the window sizes.

### Three controls
- **Filter** narrows the list by person, client, project, due date (including a custom range) or status. The number on the button shows how many filters are on. **Clear filters** removes them all.
- **Group and sort** groups the list by due date, client, **month step (4th/14th/24th)** or status, then sorts inside each group by due date, priority, status or name. Pick a sort again to flip the order. **Reset view** puts the list back the way it started.
- **Saved views** keeps a filter, group and sort under a name. Pick a saved view to switch back in one click, or set one as the default.

### Add a task in plain words
Type a task in the add line at the top of the list, for example "Call Acme about payroll tomorrow for Jesse", and press **Enter**. The line under the box shows what will be saved: the title, and any client, date or person found in the words. A name that matches more than one client or person stays in the title; press **Shift+Enter** to open the full New task form instead. **Esc** clears the box.

### Today at a glance
In the Slate app at full size, with no task open, the right side shows **Today at a glance**:
- Counts for **Overdue**, **Due this week** and **Waiting on client**, for tasks assigned to the person signed in. Admins also see **Waiting for review**. Click a count to filter the list to those tasks.
- **Start here**: three tasks to begin with, overdue first, then the soonest due.
- A small add box that works like the add line.

Opening a task replaces the panel with the task. Closing the task brings the panel back.

### The open task
The top of an open task holds **Mark done** and the main fields: assignee, due date, client, project, priority and status. The description, subtasks, checklist, files and comments follow.

Less used fields (start date, section, type, reminder, repeat, sharing, review, dependencies, custom fields and the SOP section) sit under **More details**. The number on **More details** shows how many of them are filled in. Slate remembers whether More details was left open.
