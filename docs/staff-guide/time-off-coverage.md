Tell the team when you're out, and make sure each of your clients has a **backup bookkeeper** who covers while you're away.

### Add your time off

1. Open **Team › People** (`#/team/people`) and find the **My time off** card.
2. Click **+ Add time off**.
3. Pick the **First day off** and **Last day off**. Add a **Note** if you like. Every staff member can see it.
4. Click **Save time off**.

Changed plans? Click **Cancel** next to the dates, then **Yes, cancel it**. Cancelled time off is kept in the records but disappears from the app.

The whole team can see who's out. Clients never see any of it.

### The "Out until" tag

While you're out, an **Out until {date}** tag shows next to your name:

- On the client's **Overview**, in **Key dates and coverage** (next to **Bookkeeper** or **Backup**).
- In the [Inbox](#/help/inbox) details panel, next to **Bookkeeper**.

If you see it on a client's bookkeeper, send the work to the **Backup** instead.

### Set a client's backup

1. Open the client's **Overview** and click **Edit dates and coverage**.
2. Pick someone in **Backup bookkeeper (covers when they're out)**. It lists active staff.
3. Click **Save**.

A backup doesn't automatically get into the client. If they aren't assigned to it, they can [request temporary access](#/help/temporary-access), or an admin can give it from the **Coverage** tab.

### Coverage tab (admins)

**Team › People** shows **Who's out** now and in the next 30 days, and **My clients' backups**. For each person:

- Their dates and note, and a pill: **All covered** or **{n} clients not covered**.
- Each of their clients, the client's **Backup**, and **Backup's access**: **Has access (admin)**, **Has access (assigned)**, **Temporary access**, **No access**, **No backup set** or **Backup isn't active staff**.
- **Give access for this time off**: gives the backup temporary access to that client right away. It ends the day after the time off ends. It shows up under [temporary access](#/help/temporary-access) like any other grant and can be ended early there.
- **Set a backup**: opens the client's overview so you can pick one.

Admins can also **+ Add time off** for anyone, and cancel anyone's.

When nobody is out, the tab says **Nobody has time off in the next 30 days.**
