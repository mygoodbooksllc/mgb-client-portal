Admins run quarterly reviews from **Reviews → Team status**. For how reviews work for the person being reviewed, see [How do quarterly reviews work?](#/help/quarterly-reviews).

Have your lawyer or HR advisor review the review PDF and the keep-forever retention before the first cycle.

### Open a cycle

1. Go to **Reviews → Team status** and click **Open a review cycle** (the form shows on its own when there's no cycle yet).
2. Pick the year, the **quarter being reviewed**, the open date and the due date. Reviews cover the quarter that just ended, so the form starts on it (Q3 in October, Q4 of last year in January), with the due date at the end of the next month.
3. Check who's being reviewed. Everyone who isn't an admin is checked to start.
4. Each person gets a reviewer. It starts as the **Default reviewer** (Jesse, unless you change it), and you can pick a different admin per person. Nobody can review themselves.
5. Click **Open the Q… review**. Everyone picked gets an email, and so do their reviewers.

The **Open a review cycle** button only shows when no quarter is open.

### While it's open

The Team status page shows, for each person: reviewer, self-review status, the reviewer's review status, the survey, the meeting and signature status, and the reviewer's total. Above it: how many self-reviews, reviewer reviews and signed reviews are in. Below it: team averages for the year per section, out of 5.

- **Send reminder** emails everyone who still has something to do. A reminder also goes out on its own 3 days before the due date.
- **Add people** adds someone to the open cycle.
- **Change a reviewer** with the dropdown in the Reviewer column. This works until the reviewer's review is submitted. You can add a reason; it's kept in the review's history. Once the person has started their self-review, a former reviewer can't be put back on it, because other admins can see self-reviews and they could have looked while they were off it.
- **Close unsigned** closes one person's review without signatures. A reason is required, and it's kept.

Reviewers fill in their reviews from **Reviews → Reviews I'm giving**. The blind rule still applies to the reviewer: you can't see someone's self-review scores until your own review of them is submitted.

Every other admin can click **Open** on Team status to follow a review as it happens. The **In progress** view shows both forms' scores and comments so far, drafts included. The person and their reviewer still can't see each other's side until both are in. An admin who is being reviewed never sees the reviewer's side early.

### Reopen and addenda

Before anyone has signed, an admin can reopen a submitted form from the review's page so its author can change it. A reason is required. The comparison goes back to hidden until the form is submitted again, and the author gets an email.

Once someone has signed, forms can't be reopened. After a review locks, add corrections as an addendum on the review's page. The signed review itself never changes.

### Close the cycle

Click **Close cycle**. The confirmation lists every review that isn't signed yet. Those are marked **Closed unsigned** and kept, read-only, and their open action steps carry forward. Closing also stops survey answers for that quarter.

### Signed PDFs and Google Drive

When the second signature lands, the review locks and its PDF saves to the **~ MGB: Staff Reviews** Shared Drive as *{Staff Name} / {YYYY} Q{n} Review.pdf*. Exporting again saves a new version of the same file. Team status shows **In Drive**, **Waiting for Drive setup** or **Drive export failed** under each signed review, and the portal tries again about once an hour. The review's page also has an **Export to Drive** button. The **Google Drive** card at the bottom of Team status shows whether the portal can reach the drive. Staff never get Drive links; they download their own PDFs from **History**. See [How do I set up Google Drive for staff reviews?](#/help/review-drive-setup).

When a review locks, the team member and the reviewer each get one **review final** email: the final scores, the change from that person's last locked review (by any reviewer) and the agreed action steps. Exporting again or adding an addendum doesn't send it again. Reviews that locked before October 19, 2026 got the older "signed and locked" email and don't get a second one.

### Year-end summary

**Reviews → Year-end** (admins only):

1. Pick the person and year. The page shows their scores by quarter and a draft summary (strongest area, focus area, alignment) written from the year's scores.
2. Edit the text and click **Save draft**, or **Confirm summary** when it's ready.
3. Once confirmed, the person can see it and download it from their History, and you can **Download PDF** or **Export to Drive** (*{Staff Name} / {YYYY} Year-End Summary.pdf*).

Editing a confirmed summary puts it back to draft until it's confirmed again.

### Good to know

- Reviews, signatures and survey answers are kept permanently. Nothing is deleted.
- Clients can't reach anything in Reviews.
- Notifications go by email. Google Chat notifications aren't set up yet.
