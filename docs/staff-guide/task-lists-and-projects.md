The **Slate** page (the **Slate** button in the top bar, or `#/tasks/my`) has a sidebar of lists down the left. Below about 900 pixels wide, the sidebar sits behind the **Lists** button at the top of the page.

- **Slate**: everything assigned to you. See [How do I use my Slate?](#/help/my-tasks).
- **Review**: work waiting for an admin's approval, with a count for admins. See [How does task review work?](#/help/task-review).
- **Recurring**: the templates that make monthly, quarterly, yearly and weekly tasks on their own. See [How do recurring tasks from templates work?](#/help/task-templates).
- **Search**: searches task names, descriptions and comments.
- **Projects**: shared lists for internal work. **New project** (the + next to the heading) starts one.
- **Clients**: one list for each client you can see. With more than 8, a **Find a client** box narrows the list.
- **People** (admins only): any staff member's tasks.
- **Manage projects** (admins only, under **Projects**): rename, archive or trash any project. See [How do I rename, archive or trash a project?](#/help/manage-projects).
- **No team yet** (admins only, above Clients): clients brought over from Asana that have no bookkeeper, with how many tasks each has. It hides when there are none. See *Clients with no team yet* below.

### A list

A task with subtasks has an arrow and a count (for example **2/5**). Click the row to open its subtasks right under it; click the task's name to open its details. See [How do I use my Slate?](#/help/my-tasks).

Each list shows its tasks with **Task**, **Assignee**, **Due**, **Status** and **Priority** columns, grouped into sections. Click a column heading to sort by it. Each section ends with an **Add task** row: click it, type a name and press **Enter** (**Esc** cancels), and the new task lands in that section. Each row's assignee, due date and priority change in place with a click; see [How do I use my Slate?](#/help/my-tasks).

Filter by **Assignee** (**Anyone**, **Unassigned** or one person) and **Status**, and turn on **Show done** and **Show older** to see finished tasks. On a phone the filters sit behind a **Filters** button.

### Client lists

A client's list starts with the things you reach for most:

- **Open client**, the **SOP**, whether QuickBooks is connected, and **Open Drive folder**.
- **Contacts (N)**: the client's people, with tags such as **Unsubscribed** and **Gets summaries**.
- The client's **Bookkeeper**, **Backup** and **Account Manager**.
- **New onboarding project** for a new client.

A task nobody is on shows **Unassigned** in the Assignee column with a **Claim** button. See [How do I use my Slate?](#/help/my-tasks).

Client tasks sit in sections such as **Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, **Annual** and **Occasional**.

**Who sees a client's tasks:** admins, the client's assigned bookkeeper, and a bookkeeper who is covering for them or has temporary access to that client. A private client task is seen only by its owner, its assignee, its followers and admins; a shared one by everyone who can see the client. See [Time off and coverage](#/help/time-off-coverage) and [Temporary access](#/help/temporary-access).

### Clients with no team yet (admins)

Clients brought over from Asana sometimes arrive with no bookkeeper. They're listed under **No team yet** in the sidebar until they get one.

1. Click the client under **No team yet** to open its list.
2. Click **Assign team** in the header (it only shows while the client has no bookkeeper).
3. Pick the bookkeeper and click **Assign**.

You'll see *Assigned {name} as bookkeeper.*, and how many unassigned tasks they now own. The client leaves **No team yet**, the bookkeeper joins the client's projects, and the imported tasks that were parked with nobody on them become theirs to look after. They stay **Unassigned**, so anyone on the team can **Claim** them. Tasks already assigned to someone aren't touched. No email is sent. If someone already set a team, you'll see that the client already has a team; reopen it to see who.

### Projects

Projects hold internal work that isn't for one client. Four are set up for you:

| Project | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **SOPs** | All staff (Open to all staff is on) |
| **90 Day** | All staff (Open to all staff is on) |
| **Directors** | Admins only |
| **Owners** | Admins only |

**Open to all staff** means everyone on staff can see the project and add tasks to it. Otherwise, a project is seen by its members and by admins. Only admins can switch Open to all staff on or off.

**Project colors.** Every project has a color, shown as a dot next to its name in the sidebar, at the top of the project and on its tasks in Slate. New projects get the color fewest other projects use, and the existing ones were colored once, oldest first. A project's owner or an admin can change it: **Project options › Change color** on the project's page, or **Color** on its row in **Manage projects**. Everyone sees the same color. The name is always beside the dot, so the color is a help, not the only clue.

**Start a project**: click **New project** in the sidebar, type a **Name**, add **Sections (optional, one per line)**, and click **Create project**. You become its owner.

**Manage a project** (its owners and admins): use **Add member** to add someone as a **Member** or as an **Owner (can manage the project)**, and the **Project options** menu to **Rename** it, edit its **Sections**, **Change color**, switch **Open to all staff** (admins), **Archive project** or **Move to trash**. Archiving takes it out of the sidebar and makes its tasks read-only. **Move to trash** hides the project and its tasks from every list until an admin restores it from **Manage projects**; it's refused while a task in the project is waiting on a client request. Nothing is deleted either way. See [How do I rename, archive or trash a project?](#/help/manage-projects).

### Another person's tasks (admins)

Under **People**, pick a staff member to see their list. It's useful for covering or checking someone's load. Only admins can open it; anyone else sees *Only admins can see another person's list.*

The toolbar has a **Group by** switch: **Default** (the list's sections) or **Client**, which groups the person's tasks one client at a time, clients A to Z, then **No client**, then test and internal clients. The portal remembers your choice.

### Troubleshooting

- **A client is missing from the sidebar.** You only see clients you're assigned to, covering, or have temporary access to. Ask an admin.
- **"We couldn't find that list."** The project was archived or moved to the trash, or you don't have access to it. An admin can restore a trashed one from **Manage projects**. Click **Go to Slate**.
- **"There's already a project with that name. Try another name."** Project names must be unique.
