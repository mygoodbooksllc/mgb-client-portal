A client contact is someone at the client who answers your requests without a portal sign-in: a treasurer, a pastor, a board member. When you **Ask client** and tick a contact, they get an email with a link that opens just that one request.

### Add, edit or remove a contact

1. Open the client's tasks drawer (the client's task list) and expand **Client contacts** at the bottom.
2. Click **+ Add contact**. Enter a **Name**, an **Email**, a **Role** (Treasurer, Pastor, Board, Finance, Other) and optional **Notes**.
3. Leave **Can answer requests by email link** ticked, then click **Save**.

**Edit** changes any of those. **Remove** takes the contact off the client. You can also add a contact from the **Ask client** window with **+ Add contact**. A contact belongs to one client, and the same email can't be added twice.

> **Unticking Can answer requests by email link, or removing a contact, stops their open links at once.** Use it when a link went to the wrong person or someone leaves the church.

### What the contact sees

The email has a **Reply or upload** button. It opens a plain MyGoodBooks page with the request's title, your message, who asked and when it's needed. They type an answer, attach files if you asked for one (up to 5 files, 25 MB each: PDF, pictures, spreadsheets, Word or text) and click **Send**. They see *Thanks, {your first name} has it* and the request closes. Files go to the client's Google Drive folder like any other upload.

- A link works for **14 days** and for **one answer**. After that the page says the link has closed and offers an email to MyGoodBooks.
- **Email again** sends a fresh link; the older link stops working.
- Reminder emails have a **Stop reminders** link. If they click it, reminders stop for every open request for that client that lists them (you'll see reminders paused on each).
- The page never shows anything else about the client, and the link only works for that one request.

### Troubleshooting

- **"Link not recognized" or "This link was replaced".** They used an older email. Send **Email again** and ask them to use the newest one.
- **"Too many tries".** The page was opened or sent too many times in a short while. Waiting a few minutes fixes it.
- **Nothing arrived.** Request emails only go to clients the owner has switched on (**Ask client emails live** plus the pilot list on the Emails page). For any other client, sending is a dry run (or a test copy to you for a test client). See [How do I ask a client something from a task?](#/help/ask-client).
