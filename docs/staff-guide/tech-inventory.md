**Team › Tech** (`#/team/tech`) keeps track of the equipment the firm gives you, and lets you ask for more. It replaces the old Tech Inventory spreadsheet.

### For everyone

**My tech** opens first.

- **Request hardware**: in **What do you need?** pick an item from the standard setup or type your own, choose **Low**, **Normal** or **High**, say why, and click **Send request**. An email goes to admin@mygoodbooks.org. Your request shows under **My requests** with its status: **Open**, **Ordered**, **Fulfilled** or **Declined**. While it's still Open you can **Withdraw** it.
- **My items**: what you have now. Click **Log an item** to add something (category, item, serial, condition, date you got it, notes). **Edit** fixes a typo; **Archive** takes it off your list when you give it back or it breaks.

**Standard setup** is the list of what every team member gets, with links and prices. Items marked **Essential** are the ones everyone should have.

### For admins

The four tiles at the top are clickable: **Open requests** (open or ordered), **Items**, **Laptops** and **Missing essentials** (people missing at least one essential).

- **Requests**: everyone's requests. Change the status in the row; **Archive** files a request away. **New request** sends one of your own.
- **Inventory**: every item. Search, filter by person, or **Log an item** for anyone.
- **Roster**: one row per active staff member with their laptop, item count, open requests and which essentials they're missing. Click a name to see their items. A person's laptop counts for the laptop essential whatever the brand.
- **Directory**: name and email from **Team › Members**, plus personal email, phones and home address. **Edit** a row to update it. Only admins can see this.
- **Standard setup**: **Add item**, **Edit** and **Archive**.

**Export to Google Sheet** saves a new sheet to the admin-only Shared Drive (folder "Tech inventory") with Requests, Inventory, Roster, Standard setup and Directory tabs, and gives you an **Open the sheet** link. Each export is a new dated file.

### Good to know

- Nothing is ever deleted. **Archive** hides a row; **Show archived** lists archived rows, and **Restore** puts one back.
- People come from **Team › Members**. To add someone to Tech, add them there first (with their @mygoodbooks.org email).
