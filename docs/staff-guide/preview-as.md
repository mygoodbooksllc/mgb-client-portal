On any client page, the sidebar has two preview controls. Neither changes anything for the client.

### Preview as a client user

1. In the sidebar, open **Preview as**.
2. Pick a person (listed as *Name — role*). Choose "{Your name} (full access)" to go back.
3. A bar appears: "Previewing as {name} — {role}. This is exactly what they see when they sign in."
4. Click **Exit preview** when you're done.

While previewing, staff-only things are hidden: the staff bar, the Client overview and the staff note icons.

### Preview a different plan

**Preview plan** lets you see the portal as it looks on another plan. It only changes your browser and doesn't change what the client pays. Choose **Actual plan** to go back.
