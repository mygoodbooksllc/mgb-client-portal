On any client page there are two preview controls. Neither changes anything for the client.

### Preview as a client user

1. Click your initials at the top right of the page, then **Preview as a client user**. (On a phone, open the menu and use **Preview as** instead.)
2. Pick a person (listed as *Name — role*).
3. A bar appears: "Previewing as {name} — {role}. This is exactly what they see when they sign in."
4. Click **Exit preview** when you're done.

While previewing, staff-only things are hidden: the top bar, the staff bar, the Client overview and the staff note icons.

### Preview a different plan

**Preview plan** (in the client's sidebar) lets you see the portal as it looks on another plan. It only changes your browser and doesn't change what the client pays. Choose **Actual plan** to go back.
