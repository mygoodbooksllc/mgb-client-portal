The Slate app has a **Settings** page of its own for how Slate looks and works for you. Open it with the **gear** in the Slate app's top bar, or from the portal with **?** → **Slate settings** on the Slate page (it opens the Slate app on its Settings page). The address is `#/tasks/settings` in the Slate app.

These settings are **just for you, in this browser**. Nobody else's Slate changes, and another computer keeps its own.

### Default view

How Slate opens:

- **Group by**: how your list is grouped (due date, client, status, part of month and so on).
- **Sort**: **Default order** or a column, with **Ascending** or **Descending**.
- **Quick view**: a chip that is on when Slate opens, or **None**.
- **Show done** and **Show older done**.

The view bar on your list saves the same choice, so changing it there changes it here too. **Reset to default** goes back to grouping by due date with no sort and no chip.

### Window and layout

- **Open Slate in**: **Its own window** or **The Desk panel**. **Open Slate app** in the portal uses this. If the Desk is off for you, the window opens.
- **Row density**: **Comfortable** or **Compact** rows.

### Keyboard shortcuts

- **Quick keys on** turns the single-letter keys on or off. With it off, **Ctrl+Z** (**Cmd+Z** on a Mac) undo still works.
- The card lists every key Slate knows, so you don't have to remember them.

### Undo

Shows how many changes Slate keeps for **Undo** and for how long. This is the same for everyone and can't be changed here. See [How do I use the Slate app?](#/help/tasks-own-window).

### Task notifications

A table of the task emails and bell alerts you can get. The switches themselves live in the portal, under **Settings › Notifications**: click **Open Notifications in the portal** to go straight there.

### Help

- **Replay the Slate tour** runs **Slate, start to finish** again. See [How do I take the Slate tour?](#/help/slate-tour).
- **Open the staff guide** opens Slate's help in your portal window.
