Bookkeepers only see the clients assigned to them. If you need to cover someone else's client (for example during a vacation), request temporary access. It ends on its own when the time is up.

### Request access

1. Open the client picker (**Choose a client**) at the top left of the page.
2. Scroll to **Other clients**. These are locked.
3. Click **Request access** on the client you need.
4. Fill in **Why do you need access?** (required), for example *covering month-end close while their bookkeeper is out*.
5. Choose **How long**: **1 day**, **1 week** (default) or **30 days**.
6. Click **Send request**.

The client shows **Pending** in the picker until someone decides. Click **Cancel** there if you no longer need it.

### Who approves it

An admin or the client's own bookkeeper. They see the request on **Today**, in **Needs you** ("{who} wants {client} for {duration}"), and click **Approve** or **Deny**.

The same card lists **Active temporary access**, where an admin or the bookkeeper can **Revoke** access at any time.

### While you have access

- The client shows **Temporary · Xd left** in the picker.
- A note at the top of the client's pages says **Temporary access · expires in …**.
- The account menu (your initials, top right) lists it too: **Temporary access: {client} · …**. Temporary *admin* access shows there as **Temporary admin access · expires …**.
- When you're done, click **Give up access**. You can always request again later.

### Temporary admin tour

Temporary *admin* access is different: an admin gives it to a bookkeeper from **Team › Members** (1 hour, 1 day or 1 week), and it opens some admin pages instead of a client. See [Staff and access](#/help/staff-management).

- A short **Temporary admin tour** starts by itself the next time you open the portal, once for each grant. If you're given access again later, it starts again, because it's a new grant.
- It points out what's new for you: **Team › Hours** (hours and tasks by person and client, and hours budgets; fees and margin stay with admins), **Reply times** (you see your own replies and your own clients), **Feedback** (you can read only the reports you sent yourself), **Members** (the staff list, read-only for you) and **Settings › Firm settings** (**Task templates**, **Client roster**, **Usage stats** and **Developer tools**). About a minute.
- The last step says when your access ends. At that time these pages go away by themselves. **Emails**, **QuickBooks usage**, the **Audit log** and money figures stay with admins.
- While your access lasts, run it again from **?** → **Temporary admin tour**, from **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac), or from **Settings › Help**.

### Troubleshooting

- **"You already have a pending request for this client."** Wait for the approver, or cancel the pending one first.
- **"Add a reason so the approver knows why you need access."** The reason can't be empty.
- **"Your session has expired. Sign in again and retry."** Sign out, sign back in and try again.
- **I don't see "Other clients".** Admins already see every client, so they don't have this section. Test clients are never listed there.
- **I don't see the Temporary admin tour in the ? menu.** It only shows while your temporary admin access lasts, and not during an admin's **View as**.
