On QuickBooks clients, the client can click **Ask** next to any transaction on their **Bank** page. They say what they think it was for (picking a category or typing their own), add an optional note, and send it. Each transaction can have one open question at a time.

### Where you see open questions

- **Inbox**: the question arrives as a message in the client's thread ("Question about a transaction: ..."), so it shows as waiting on you, sends the usual new-message email, and appears in the bell under client messages. The client panel beside the thread also lists the client's open **Transaction questions**.
- **Client overview**: an **{n} open questions** chip in **Open for this client**. It turns red when the oldest question has waited more than a week. With one question it opens that question. With more it opens **Bank** filtered to **Open questions**.
- **Bank** tab: a number on the tab counts the open questions. Inside, the transaction row shows a **Question** tag, and the **Open questions** filter shows only those rows. If some questions are on transactions older than the list covers, a line says *{n} more open questions are on older transactions* and lists them underneath. Questions on a transaction Bank isn't showing at all (for example one voided in QuickBooks) are listed there too, under *{n} open questions are on transactions Bank isn't showing*.

### How to resolve one

1. Open the question from any of the places above to see the transaction, the category the client suggested, their note, and who asked.
2. **Recategorize the transaction in QuickBooks yourself.** MyGoodBooks never changes QuickBooks; it only reads from it.
3. Back in MyGoodBooks, add a short reply if you like (for example *Moved to Building Repairs, thanks!*) and click **Mark resolved**.

Resolving closes the question and posts a reply in the client's thread, so they get the usual message email. The new category shows on the Bank tab after the client's next QuickBooks sync, which depends on their plan: every hour on Pro, monthly (on the 15th) on Basic.

### Good to know

- Only staff with access to the client can see or resolve their questions. Clients see only their own organization's questions.
- Questions can't be deleted, and a resolved question can't be reopened. If the client asks again, they send a new question.
- If you don't agree with the client's suggestion, reply in the thread to explain, then mark it resolved once it's settled.
- Questions you ask from **Ledger review** in the portal show on the client's **Home** under *Needs you* and at the top of their **Bank** page. A client user without the Bank page answers right from Home. Once they've answered, they see their own words only (*Your bookkeeper asked* / *You answered*), never your name or email.
