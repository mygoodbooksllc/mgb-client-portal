A client's **SOP** (standard operating procedure) explains how their books are run, so anyone on the team can step in. SOPs are **staff-only** and every change is saved in history.

Not the same as the **SOPs** project on the Slate page: that's a shared task list, open to all staff, for work on writing and updating SOPs. See [How do client task lists and projects work?](#/help/task-lists-and-projects).

### Open a client's SOP

- The client's **SOP** tab: open the client and click **SOP** in the tab row (after **Reports**).
- **Work › Notes & SOPs → SOPs** card (`#/work/notes`; it used to sit under Work › Slate): search for the client and click it. Use **Change client** to switch.
- A client's task list (on the Slate page) has an **SOP** button at the top. A task's details can link to one **SOP section**: **View SOP section** shows it without leaving the task. See [What's in a task's details?](#/help/task-details).
- Or **Client details → SOP** (the client's **Client setup** gear → **Access and details** → **Client details**) shows it read-only. To change it, open the client's **SOP** tab or **Work › Notes & SOPs → SOPs**.

### The seven sections

1. **Access & logins**: where credentials live. **Never paste passwords here.** Write where they're kept instead.
2. **Bank & credit card feeds**
3. **Monthly close steps**
4. **Payroll**
5. **Recurring bills & vendors**
6. **Reporting & deliverables**
7. **Quirks, contacts & preferences**

The top of the SOP shows how many are filled in, for example "4 of 7 sections filled".

### Keeping it current

Under that line, a tag says **Last reviewed {n} days ago**. It counts from whichever is later: the last edit to any section, or the last time someone clicked **Mark as still accurate**. Hover over it to see both dates and who marked it.

- Green: reviewed in the last 180 days.
- Red, with "Over 180 days. Give it a read and fix anything out of date.": the SOP is **stale**.

Read it through. Fix anything that's changed. If it's all still right, click **Mark as still accurate**. The tag goes back to **Last reviewed today**. You can do this from **Client details → SOP** too, without opening the SOP tab.

Stale SOPs also show on **Today** under **Also needs you** with an **SOP** tag, in the **Clients** page's **Needs attention** filter, and in Client health. An SOP with nothing written yet isn't counted as stale (Client health flags it as "No SOP written yet" instead).

### Edit a section

1. Click **Add** (empty section) or **Edit**.
2. Type your text. Start lines with **-** for bullets or **1.** for numbered steps.
3. Click **Save**. The section shows who edited it last and when.

### History and restoring

Click **History** on a section to see **Earlier versions**. **View** shows an old version; **Restore** puts it back (the current text stays in history too).

### If someone else edited at the same time

You'll see **"Updated by … — review their version"** with both versions side by side. Choose **Load theirs** to start from their text, or **Keep mine (overwrite)**. Nothing is lost: their version stays in History either way.

### Sharing an SOP

- **Print** opens a printable copy (allow pop-ups if nothing happens).
- **Copy as text** copies the whole SOP so you can paste it elsewhere.
