Drag a file from your computer and drop it **anywhere** in the staff app. You don't need to open the Documents page first.

While you drag, the page shows **Drop to add to {client}'s documents**. Let go and a short box opens:

1. **Client**: the client you have open is picked for you. On Today and other staff pages, pick one from the **Clients** page or the client picker.
2. **Folder**: pick one of the client's folders, or leave it on **No folder**.
3. **Document type** and **Year**: which Google Drive folder the file goes in (*{client} / 2026 / Bank statements*). It starts on **Other** and this year. Shown once Google Drive is connected; see [Where are client files stored?](#/help/google-drive-files).
4. **Visible to client**: **off by default**.
   - **Off**: only MyGoodBooks staff can see the file. It shows on the client's **Documents** page with a dashed **Staff only** tag, and the client never sees it.
   - **On**: everyone on the client's team can see it in their Documents.
5. Click **Upload**. You'll see *Added 1 file to {client}'s documents (staff only).* or *…Their team can see it.*

You can drop up to 10 files at a time. Each file can be up to 25 MB, and must be a PDF, image (PNG, JPEG, HEIC), CSV, text, Excel or Word file. Files that don't fit are crossed out in the box with the reason, and the rest still upload.

Files are stored in the firm's Google Drive (**~ MGB: Client Files (Portal)**). On the Documents page, staff pick the Drive folder next to **Drive folder** before uploading, and each Drive file's row has **Open in Drive** (staff only). If the upload box ever says *File storage isn't connected yet; tell the owner.*, Drive has been disconnected; tell the owner.

### Folders

Every client starts with five folders: **Bank statements**, **Financial statements**, **Tax**, **Receipts** and **Payroll**. Folders are shared, so the client and every staff member see the same ones.

- To file a document, use the **Folder** menu in its row.
- To make a folder, pick **+ New folder…** at the bottom of a row's **Folder** menu, type a name and click **Create and move**. The document goes straight into it. **+ New Folder** at the end of the folder bar works too.
- Clicking **×** on a folder deletes it for everyone. Its documents go back to **Unfiled**; nothing is deleted.
- Clients can add, use and delete folders too. They never see staff-only files, whichever folder those are in.

### The Staff only folder

On a client's **Documents** page, staff see two extra folders next to **All Documents**: **Staff only** and **Trash**. Clients never see either.

- **Staff only** lists every file the client can't see. While it's open, the upload box says **Add a staff-only file**, and anything you upload there is staff only.
- Staff-only files also show in **All Documents** with a dashed **Staff only** tag, and **Visible to** says **Staff only**.

### Trash a document

Click the trash can at the end of a document's row (staff only). The file or link moves to the **Trash** folder and the client can't see it any more. You'll see *Moved {name} to Trash. You can restore it from the Trash folder.*

- Open **Trash** and click **Restore** to put it back where it was, in the same folder.
- Trash never empties on its own for links and older files. Items stay there until someone restores them or deletes them forever.
- **Google Drive files** also go to Google Drive's trash, which Drive empties after **30 days**. Restore before then. You'll see *Restore it within 30 days, before Drive empties its trash.*
- On the client's **Documents** tab, **Move to Trash** on a shared link does the same thing. Restore it from the Documents page.
- The sample documents on prototype clients can't be trashed.

### Delete a document forever

Only staff can do this, and only from **Trash**. Google Drive files don't have this button: the portal never deletes them, and Drive empties its own trash after 30 days.

1. Open **Trash** and click **Delete forever** on the item.
2. A box asks *Delete "{name}" forever?* Click **Delete forever** to confirm, or **Cancel**.

You'll see *Deleted {name} forever.* This can't be undone: the file is removed from storage (or the link is removed), and no one can restore it.

### Places that take drops themselves

Dropping onto the **Documents** upload box, a message thread or the **Feedback** page works the way it always has there. The drop box doesn't open on top of another open window, while you're previewing a client user, or for client users.

### Clients can drop files anywhere too

A client user with the **Documents** tab can drop a file on any page of their portal (on a Basic client's Home, Home's own drop box still handles it). On a phone they tap **+ Add a file** at the bottom left, on any page except Documents. A box called **Add to your documents** opens:

1. **Folder**: one of their folders, **No folder**, or **New folder…**.
2. **Name**: for a single file, starts as the file's name without the extension. It's the name everyone sees in Documents; the file in Google Drive keeps its own name.
3. **What it is for**: Bank statement, Credit card statement, Receipts, Invoices and bills, Tax documents or Something else, then the other document types. It picks the Google Drive folder, the same as **Document type** for staff.
4. **Who can see it**: **Everyone at {organization}** (the default), **Only these people** or **Everyone except these people**, with a list of their organization's logins. The person uploading is always on an **Only** list. Under it: *MyGoodBooks staff can always see your files.* Organizations with a single login don't see this choice.

Same limits as the Documents page: up to 10 files, 25 MB each, PDF, images, CSV, text, Excel or Word. If a file fails, the box stays open with just the failures so they can try again. Uploads show as a **New file** on the Desk's Inbox (**Files** filter) like any client upload. Nobody is emailed unless the owner turns on upload emails; then the client's account manager gets one (their own **A client uploads a requested document** choice in **Settings › Notifications** still applies).

**Who can see it, the rules:**

- A rule applies to every client login, full access included. Only MyGoodBooks staff see every file.
- Only the person who uploaded a file, or staff, can change its rule. On their Documents page the uploader sees **Only some people** or **Hidden from some people** on the row and a **Who can see** button.
- Someone left off a file doesn't see it in Documents, in search or anywhere else in the portal.
- If someone on an **Only** list loses their login, the box says so the next time the rule is opened and leaves them off. Someone on an **Everyone except** list stays hidden from the file even after their login goes, so the file never opens up to them by accident.

### More on a client's Documents page (staff)

- **Each file's ⋯** has **Rename**, **Who can see it**, **Replace with a new version**, **Copy link** and **History**.
  - **Rename** changes the name in the portal only. The Google Drive file keeps its name, shown in small gray text under the new name.
  - **Who can see it** sets the same rule clients can set. Staff can change it on any client file.
  - **Replace with a new version** uploads the new file with the same name, type, year, folder and rule, then moves the old one to **Trash**.
  - **Copy link** copies a link to the row. Anyone who opens it still needs access to this client.
  - **History** lists uploads, renames, rule changes, trash and restore, with who and when.
- **Tick files** (or the box in the header for all of them) to **Move to** a folder or **Move to Trash** together. **Clear** unticks them.
- A small tag beside the document type shows the kind of file: PDF, Excel, Word, Image and so on.
- **✎** on a folder renames it for everyone. Its files stay in it.
- **Visible to** shows a lock with **Only 2 people** or **Everyone except 1** for a file with a rule.
- When you upload on the Documents page, **Who can see: Everyone at the client** (next to **Drive folder**) sets a rule for those files. It shows once page drop is on for that client.
- In **View as**, files the person can't see (and staff-only files) are left out, and a chip says how many are hidden.

### Good to know

- To make a staff-only file visible later, download it and upload it again with **Visible to client** on.
- Signed agreements (service agreement, engagement letter, changes) go on the **Agreements** card on the Documents tab, with their signed and renewal dates. See [Client agreements](#/help/client-agreements).
