**Slate, start to finish** is a guided tour of all of Slate in seven short chapters. It is built to present to the team as well as to take on your own: big cards, one or two sentences each, and a **Chapters** list to jump around.

The tour **only looks**. It opens pages, lists and a sample task, and points at buttons, but it never creates, edits, completes, deletes or sends anything, and it never asks a client a question. Write buttons are pointed at and explained; you try them yourself afterwards. Where it opens a client, it uses **Pro Test Client**, never a real client.

### Start it

- In the portal, on any Slate page: **?** → **Slate, start to finish**. On a computer it opens the Slate app and runs the tour there. If the Slate app can't open (a phone, a pop-up blocker, or the app is off), the tour runs in the portal instead.
- In the Slate app: the avatar menu → **Take the Slate tour**, or the gear → **Help** → **Replay the Slate tour**.
- **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) and type "Slate, start to finish", or **Settings › Help** in the portal.
- After the launch, the next-visit notice has a **Take the tour** button too.

### The seven chapters

1. **Your day**: My Slate, the quick chips, **Group by** (including the 4th, 14th and 24th), sort and saved views.
2. **Client lists**: a client's list, sections, people, links, QuickBooks and the task details fields.
3. **Working a task**: adding a task, the row, opening a task, subtasks, the checklist, the thread and **Copy link**.
4. **Speed**: selecting many, copy and paste, **Undo**, Back and Forward, window sizes and the board.
5. **Team flow**: task review, approving, **Waiting on client**, reassigning and clients with no team yet.
6. **Automation**: recurring task templates and how weekends and holidays move a due date.
7. **Your setup**: Slate settings, notifications and where help lives.

### Moving around

- **Next** and **Back**, or the **Right** and **Left** arrow keys.
- **Chapters** (on the card's top line) lists the chapters; pick one to jump to its first step. The line also says which chapter and step you are on.
- **Exit**, or **Esc**, ends the tour. Nothing is saved or changed either way.
- If a step points at something that isn't on screen for you (for example an admin-only button, or a client with no team), the card shows in the middle of the screen with the same words.

While the tour is open, Slate's single-letter keys are off so a key press can't change a task.
