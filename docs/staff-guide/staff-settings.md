Your Settings page holds everything that's just about you. To open it, click your picture or initials at the top right of the page, then **Settings**. (On a phone, open the menu and tap **Settings**.) Changes save on their own a moment after you make them. You'll see **Saved** at the top when they have.

Settings are saved to your account, so they follow you to any computer you sign in on.

### Profile

- **Photo**: click **Change photo** to pick a new picture and crop it (JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 15 MB, at least 400 × 400 pixels). Everyone has a photo, so there is no remove button; an admin tidies the background afterward. See [How do I add or change my profile photo?](#/help/profile-photo).
- **Name**: the name the whole portal uses for you: the top bar, team lists and pickers, Team pages, your clients' Dashboard cards and every new message or note you post. It saves when you click out of the field or press **Enter**, then changes everywhere. Messages and notes you posted before keep the name they were sent with. Two people can't use the same name, so if yours matches a teammate's, add a middle initial or last name. A name can't include **@** or hidden characters. Name changes show in the Audit log.
- **Title** (for example "Senior bookkeeper") and **Phone**.
- **Email** is your sign-in email. Ask an admin if it needs to change.

Your photo shows in the top bar. Your name, title, phone and photo also show on the **Your bookkeeper** or **Your account manager** card on the Dashboard of every client you're the bookkeeper or account manager for, and your name and title show on each reply you send in a client's Messages page, so keep them up to date.

### Notifications

**Email me when** turns each notification email on or off:

- A client sends a message (on to start with). This goes to the client's account manager. It also covers being looped in on a conversation or @mentioned in a reply or note.
- A client uploads a requested document (on)
- Someone assigns me a task (on). Not for tasks you give yourself, recurring template tasks, or a task on a client you can't open.
- Tasks due today (off). One morning email listing your open tasks due that day. Tasks on a client you can't open are left out, and so are tasks waiting on the client.
- My feedback changes status (on)
- Staff Handbook signatures (on). When a policy needs your signature (bookkeepers) or your countersignature (the countersigner). See [How do I sign the Staff Handbook?](#/help/staff-handbook).

The emails only say what happened and link to the portal. They never include the message text or amounts. Until the firm's email sender is fully set up, these emails may not arrive yet; the **Emails** page (admins) shows what was sent.

**Show in the bell** picks what the bell in the top bar lists: client messages waiting, documents uploaded, tasks assigned to you, **Mentions on tasks** (someone @mentioned you in a task comment), month-end closes that are blocked, and shout-outs for you. Mentions show in the bell only; there's no email for them yet.

### Appearance & start page

- **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark** or **Match my computer** (follows your computer's light or dark setting). The **Light mode** / **Dark mode** item in the account menu still works as a quick switch, and it saves here too.
- **Start page**: where you land when you sign in: **Today**, **Work › Slate** (Slate inside Work) or **Last client opened**. A link you open (for example from an email) still goes straight to its page.

### Email signature

Type the sign-off you want at the bottom of emails. It's added to emails you send clients from the portal, portal invite drafts, and payment reminder drafts from a Pro client's Financial Overview. Plain text, up to 1,000 characters. A preview shows under the box.

Turn on **Include my profile photo** to show your Profile photo beside the signature in the emails the portal sends to your clients, such as new-message and reports-ready notices. The switch is greyed out until you've added a photo under **Profile**. Drafts that open in your own mail app stay text-only.

### Dashboards

Lists every board where you've moved or hidden cards (a client's Dashboard or Financial Overview). **Reset** puts that board back to the standard layout. Saved views are kept.

### Shortcuts

A list of keyboard shortcuts: **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) to search, **↑** / **↓** to move through menus and results, **Enter** to open, **Esc** to close.

**Slate shortcuts** lists the single keys for task lists and a task's details: **j** / **k** to move, **Enter** to open, **x** done, **s** status, **a** assign, **q** to add a task from any staff page, **t** to open a client's task panel, **Esc** to close and **?** for the list. They don't fire while you're typing. See [How do I use my Slate?](#/help/my-tasks).

### Help

Start a tour (the **Slate tour** is here too), open the Staff guide or **Send feedback**. The last row, **Tips while you learn**, is the switch for the small first-30-days tips: it shows how many days are left, and turning it off and on again starts another 30 days. See [How do tips work?](#/help/tips).

### Firm settings (admins)

The firm-wide admin pages, in three groups. Click a row to open the page. Search (**Ctrl+K** / **⌘K**) jumps to them too.

- **People and work**: **Task templates** (it opens **Slate › Recurring**) and **Client roster**.
- **Email and QuickBooks**: **Emails**, with a chip showing whether sending works (**Domain verified**, **Domain pending**, **Needs attention** or **Not checked yet**), and **QuickBooks usage and limits**, with this month's **% used**. It opens the usage card on the Team page.
- **Insight and records**: **Usage stats**, **Audit log** and **Developer tools**.

- **Late tasks** (a card on the same page): **Count late tasks in scores and reports**. **Off by default** (a grace period while everyone settles into Slate). While it is off, late and overdue tasks are not counted in the performance score, client health, on-time rates, the weekly digest or the Team page's Overdue columns, and the review rubric doesn't hold them against anyone. Filing deadlines are not tasks and keep counting. **Only the owner** can turn it on; everyone else sees it greyed out with a note. When the owner turns it on, today becomes its start date: only tasks due on or after that day count, so the imported backlog never counts against anyone. Each change is recorded in the audit log.
- **Month rhythm** (a card on the same page, **admins only**): the three days each month when Slate's monthly tasks come due, **Check in** (the 4th), **Send reports** (the 14th) and **Follow up** (the 24th), all for work on the previous month. Pick each day (1st to 28th; each must come after the one before) and what happens when a date falls on a weekend or firm holiday: **Move to the next business day** (the default), **Move to the business day before** or **Keep the date**. Click **Save rhythm**. **Use 4th, 14th, 24th** puts the standard days back in the form (save to keep them). Until an admin saves, Slate uses the standard 4th, 14th, 24th. The card shows the current days and who changed them last; every change is recorded. For now the card only stores the dates: monthly rhythm tasks are not turned on yet, so saving changes nothing for staff today. Once they are on, a change applies to monthly tasks made from then on; tasks already made keep their dates.
- **Possible duplicates** (Ledger review settings, **Team › Ledger review**): **Smart (fewer false alarms)** or **Strict (flag every same-date, same-amount match)**. Smart is the default. This is the firm default for every client; an admin can set a different choice for one client on that client's Ledger review tab. Admins only, and every change is written to the Audit log (*Ledger review* group). A change to Smart applies on the next Pull; Strict brings hidden flags back right away. See [How does Ledger review work?](#/help/ledger-review).

Staff with temporary admin access see Task templates (read-only), Client roster, Usage stats and Developer tools. **Team › Feedback** stays admin-only.

### Sign out

Click your picture or initials at the top right of the page, then **Sign out**. The Settings page also has **Sign out** at its top right. (On a phone, open the menu and tap **Sign out**.)

### Troubleshooting

- **Everything is greyed out with a note about View as.** An admin using **View as** sees their own Settings, read-only ("These are your settings, not {name}'s. Exit View as to change them."), so nothing they change there affects the person they're viewing as. Exit View as first.
- **It says "Couldn't save".** Check your connection and change the setting again.
- **My photo didn't upload.** It must be PNG, JPEG or WebP and under 2 MB.
- **A client's Dashboard doesn't show my card.** The cards show for the client's assigned bookkeeper and account manager. Check **Key dates and coverage** on the Client overview. If you're both, there's one card, **Your account manager and bookkeeper**. The client may also have hidden the card with **Customize dashboard**. Pro clients with the Financial Overview as their Dashboard don't show the cards.
