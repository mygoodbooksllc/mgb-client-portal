Everyone on the team gets a short **onboarding checklist**: the first things to learn and do at MyGoodBooks. Each step can link to the Staff guide article that explains it.

### Work through your checklist

- The **Your onboarding** card on **Home** lists your steps, with a bar showing how many you've done.
- Tick a step when you've done it. Untick it if you ticked it by mistake.
- Click **Read the guide** next to a step to open the article for it.
- The card goes away by itself once every step is ticked.
- Click **Hide this card** if you don't need it (for example, you've been here a while). Hiding can't be undone from Home, so ask an admin if you want it back.
- You can also move or hide the card with **Customize dashboard**, like any other Home card.

### For admins: Team → Onboarding

Open **Team → Onboarding** (`#/team/onboarding`). Only admins see this tab.

- **Progress** lists every staff member with their bar, **Done** or "N of M", and when they last ticked a step (or **Not started**). "Hid the card" means they clicked **Hide this card**.
- Click a name to see their steps. You can tick or untick a step for them (say, after you shadowed a close together).
- **Steps** is the checklist everyone gets:
  - **+ Add a step**: a title, an optional Staff guide article (the last part of its Help link, for example `month-end-close`) and optional details.
  - **Edit** changes a step. **↑** and **↓** change the order.
  - **Retire** takes a step off everyone's checklist but keeps who finished it. Retired steps are listed underneath; click **Restore** to bring one back.

Nothing on this checklist is ever deleted, and clients never see any of it.
