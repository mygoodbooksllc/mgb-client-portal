A **shout-out** is a short public thank-you to a teammate. The whole team can see them.

### Give one

1. On **Home**, find the **Shout-outs** card. (If you don't see it, click **Customize dashboard** and add it.)
2. Click **+ Give a shout-out**.
3. Pick **Who** it's for. You can also pick a **Client** it was about (optional).
4. Write what they did, up to 500 characters, and click **Send shout-out**.

It shows at the top of the feed right away.

### What the other person sees

They get a notice in the bell in the top bar: **Shout-out from** your name, with the first line of your note. No email is sent. They can turn these notices off in **Settings → Notifications → Show in the bell → Shout-outs for me**.

### The feed

The card lists shout-outs from the last 90 days, newest first. Click **Show all** to see more than six. A client tag only shows if you can open that client.

### Hide one

- You can **Hide** shout-outs you wrote. Admins can hide any.
- A hidden shout-out disappears for everyone else. You (and admins) still see it greyed out, marked **Hidden**, with **Show again** to undo.
- Shout-outs are never deleted.

### In quarterly reviews

When a reviewer fills in a quarterly review, the **Appreciation** box shows **Shout-outs this quarter** for that person, for reference. They aren't copied into the review and don't affect the score. Hidden shout-outs aren't shown. See [How do quarterly reviews work?](#/help/quarterly-reviews).
