Click **Create** at the top of the left nav, under the logo. With a client open, the menu is headed **Add for {client}** and everything in it is filled in for that client. Clients never see it.

On a computer the nav button is a gold round **+** with the word **Create** under it (a full-width **Create** button when the nav is expanded). On a phone the left nav is a menu, so **Create** is the round **+** in the top bar, next to the bell. It is the same menu either way. In the Slate app window, **Create** is at the top of Slate's icon rail.

You can also press **q** on any staff page, as long as you aren't typing in a box, to add a task. With a client's task panel open, **q** adds the task to that client.

| Menu item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Task** (**q**) | Opens quick add, with this client already picked. |
| **Note** | Saves a staff note on this client. |
| **Request a document** | Asks the client to upload a file. |
| **Upload a file** | Takes you to this client's **Documents** page, on the upload box. Pick the Drive folder, then the file. |
| **Message** | Opens the chat drawer on this client's people, without leaving the page. |
| **Start from a template** | Opens the **New project** form for this client with the **Start from** list ready. It shows when Slate plus is on for you. |

On a staff page with no client open (Today, Work, Team and so on), the menu is headed **Add** and has **Task**. With the Desk additions on it also has **Message a client**, **Ask client…** (a question or request with no task attached) and **Request time off**; **Ask client…** and **Request time off** show with a client open too. See [How do I ask a client something from a task?](#/help/ask-client) and [How do I add time off and make sure my clients are covered?](#/help/time-off-coverage).

*Admins only:* below a line and a small **Admin** heading, **Create** also has **Invite a client login** (with a client open) and **Add a client**, which open the Client roster, **Start a team review**, which opens **HR › Reviews**, and **Invite staff**, which opens **HR › Members**. These only take you to the page; nothing is created until you finish there.

In the Slate app window, **Create** has Slate's own rows: **Task**, **New project** (with Slate plus) and **Recurring task**, and no Admin group. The Desk's rail no longer has a **+** of its own; **Create** replaces it. See [How do I use the Desk?](#/help/workbench).

### Task

The **Add a task** box opens. Type the task in one line, the way you would say it, for example *Send the payroll summary for Sierra re Example Church by Friday*. The portal picks out who, which client and when, and shows it on a **Will add** line under the box before you save anything.

- **Who:** *for Sierra*, *assign to Sierra* or **@sierra** assigns it by first name (or full name); *to Sierra* offers a **Did you mean** button instead, because *Reply to Sierra* may not mean she does it. *For me* and **@me** mean you.
- **Which client:** *for Example Church*, *re Example Church* or *client Example Church* picks the client, when the name ends the line or opens it (*re Example Church: statements*); a dash works too (*statements - Example Church*). Type the whole name; a start of a name such as *for Example* only offers a **Did you mean** button.
- **When:** *Friday*, *tomorrow*, *next week*, *by Friday*, *due the 15th*, *the 15th*, *end of month*, **10/31** and the other dates it already knew.
- **Priority:** **!high** or **!low**.

What it picked up shows as chips you can remove; **×** on a chip from plain words (*for Sierra*, *by Friday*) puts those words back in the title and they are not picked up again; **×** on a chip from a symbol (**@name**, **#client**, **!high**) removes the symbol. You can also set **Client**, **Due** and **Assignee** in the boxes below, and a box you set by hand wins over the line.

If a name could be two people, a person and a client, or more than one client, nothing is picked. The words stay in the title and a **Did you mean** line offers each choice as a button, with **Neither** to leave them as words. A name it doesn't know stays in the title too.

With a client picked, **Share with the client's team** is ticked, so the other staff on this client can see it. Untick it to keep it private.

Press **Enter** to add it, or **Shift+Enter** to add it and open its details. You'll see *Task added.* The task shows on the assignee's Slate and on the client's task list. More in [How do I use my Slate?](#/help/my-tasks).

### Note

Type the note and click **Add a note**. It's saved as a General staff note on this client. You'll see *Note added.*

### Request a document

Type **What you need** (for example *September bank statement*), a **Due** date and optional **Details**, then click **Request a document**. You'll see *Requested "{title}" from {client}. It's on their Documents.* The client sees it on their Documents page and can upload straight into it. You manage it on the client's **Documents** tab. More in [How do I request a document from a client?](#/help/document-requests).

### Upload a file

With a client open, **Create** → **Upload a file** takes you to that client's **Documents** page, scrolled to the upload box. Pick the Drive folder, then choose the file there. More in [How do I add, file, hide or delete a client's documents?](#/help/add-documents).

### Start from a template

With a client open, **Create** → **Start from a template** opens the **New project** form for that client with the **Start from** list ready. Pick a template, give the project a name and create it. It shows when Slate plus is on for you. More in [How do client task lists and projects work?](#/help/task-lists-and-projects).

### Message

Opens the small chat drawer for this client. Press **Esc** or click outside it to close. (During an admin's **View as**, it opens the client's **Messages** page instead.) In View as, **⌘K** searches pages and help (staff articles only) but hides the bookkeeper's private tasks, and actions that save are refused. More in [How do I message a client or a teammate?](#/help/inbox).

### Where did the Staff bar go?

The small **Staff** bar that used to sit at the top of every client page (Overview, Add task, Request document, Add note, Message) is gone. Its forms are all in **Create** now, and **Overview** is the first tab in the client's tab row. See [What's on the bar at the top of the page?](#/help/top-bar).

### Troubleshooting

- **"Couldn't save that. Please try again."** Check your connection and try again. If it keeps happening, tell an admin.
- **I don't see the Create button.** It is at the top of the left nav on a computer, and the round **+** in the top bar on a phone. Both are hidden while you use **Preview as** to look at the portal as a client user, and the round **+** hides in a very narrow window. Press **q** to add a task.
- **q doesn't do anything.** Click somewhere outside the text box first, and close any open menu or tour.
