With a client open, click **+** in the dark bar at the top of the page. The menu is headed **Add for {client}**. Clients never see it.

On a staff page with no client open (Today, Work, Team and so on), **+** says **Add** and has just **New task**. You can also press **q** on any staff page, as long as you aren't typing in a box, to add a task. With a client's task panel open, **q** adds the task to that client.

| Menu item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **New task** | Opens quick add, with this client already picked. |
| **New note** | Saves a staff note on this client. |
| **Request document** | Asks the client to upload a file. |
| **Message** | Opens the chat drawer on this client's people, without leaving the page. |

The **+** button is on phones too, next to the bell at the top of the screen.

With the Desk on, on a computer, the **+** sits at the foot of the Desk's rail on the right instead of in the top bar. It's the same menu, for the client you have open. See [How do I use the Desk?](#/help/workbench).

### New task

The **Add a task** box opens. Type the task in one line, the way you would say it, for example *Send the payroll summary for Sierra re Example Church by Friday*. The portal picks out who, which client and when, and shows it on a **Will add** line under the box before you save anything.

- **Who:** *for Sierra*, *assign to Sierra* or **@sierra** assigns it by first name (or full name); *to Sierra* offers a **Did you mean** button instead, because *Reply to Sierra* may not mean she does it. *For me* and **@me** mean you.
- **Which client:** *for Example Church*, *re Example Church* or *client Example Church* picks the client, when the name ends the line or opens it (*re Example Church: statements*); a dash works too (*statements - Example Church*). Type the whole name; a start of a name such as *for Example* only offers a **Did you mean** button.
- **When:** *Friday*, *tomorrow*, *next week*, *by Friday*, *due the 15th*, *the 15th*, *end of month*, **10/31** and the other dates it already knew.
- **Priority:** **!high** or **!low**.

What it picked up shows as chips you can remove; **×** on a chip from plain words (*for Sierra*, *by Friday*) puts those words back in the title and they are not picked up again; **×** on a chip from a symbol (**@name**, **#client**, **!high**) removes the symbol. You can also set **Client**, **Due** and **Assignee** in the boxes below, and a box you set by hand wins over the line.

If a name could be two people, a person and a client, or more than one client, nothing is picked. The words stay in the title and a **Did you mean** line offers each choice as a button, with **Neither** to leave them as words. A name it doesn't know stays in the title too.

With a client picked, **Share with the client's team** is ticked, so the other staff on this client can see it. Untick it to keep it private.

Press **Enter** to add it, or **Shift+Enter** to add it and open its details. You'll see *Task added.* The task shows on the assignee's Slate and on the client's task list. More in [How do I use my Slate?](#/help/my-tasks).

### New note

Type the note and click **Add a note**. It's saved as a General staff note on this client. You'll see *Note added.*

### Request document

Type **What you need** (for example *September bank statement*), a **Due** date and optional **Details**, then click **Request a document**. You'll see *Requested "{title}" from {client}. It's on their Documents.* The client sees it on their Documents page and can upload straight into it. You manage it on the client's **Documents** tab. More in [How do I request a document from a client?](#/help/document-requests).

### Message

Opens the small chat drawer for this client. Press **Esc** or click outside it to close. (During an admin's **View as**, it opens the client's **Messages** page instead.) More in [How do I message a client or a teammate?](#/help/inbox).

### Where did the Staff bar go?

The small **Staff** bar that used to sit at the top of every client page (Overview, Add task, Request document, Add note, Message) is gone. Its forms are all in **+** now, and **Overview** is the first tab in the client's tab row. See [What's on the bar at the top of the page?](#/help/top-bar).

### Troubleshooting

- **"Couldn't save that. Please try again."** Check your connection and try again. If it keeps happening, tell an admin.
- **I don't see the + button.** The whole top bar is hidden while you use **Preview as** to look at the portal as a client user, and **+** hides in a very narrow window. With the Desk on, look for **+** at the bottom of the Desk's rail on the right. Press **q** to add a task.
- **q doesn't do anything.** Click somewhere outside the text box first, and close any open menu or tour.
