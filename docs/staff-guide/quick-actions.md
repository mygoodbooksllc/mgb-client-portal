With a client open, click **+** in the dark bar at the top of the page. The menu is headed **Add for {client}**. Clients never see it.

| Menu item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **New task** | Adds a task for this client to My Tasks. |
| **New note** | Saves a staff note on this client. |
| **Request document** | Asks the client to upload a file. |
| **Message** | Opens the chat drawer on this client's people, without leaving the page. |

The **+** button is on phones too, next to the bell at the top of the screen.

### New task

Type the task (for example *Follow up on the missing receipts*), pick a **Due** date if you like and click **Add a task**. **Visible to everyone on this client** is ticked by default, so the other staff on this client can see it. Untick it to keep it private.

### New note

Type the note and click **Add a note**. It's saved as a General staff note on this client.

### Request document

Type **What you need** (for example *September bank statement*), a **Due** date and optional **Details**, then click **Request a document**. The client sees it on their Documents page and can upload straight into it. More in [How do I request a document from a client?](#/help/document-requests).

### Message

Opens the small chat drawer for this client. Press **Esc** or click outside it to close. (During an admin's **View as**, it opens the client's **Messages** page instead.) More in [How do I message a client or a teammate?](#/help/inbox).

### Where did the Staff bar go?

The small **Staff** bar that used to sit at the top of every client page (Overview, Add task, Request document, Add note, Message) is gone. Its forms are all in **+** now, and **Overview** is a button next to the client picker. See [What's on the bar at the top of the page?](#/help/top-bar).

### Troubleshooting

- **"Couldn't save that. Please try again."** Check your connection and try again. If it keeps happening, tell an admin.
- **I don't see the + button.** It only shows when a client is open, and the whole top bar is hidden while you use **Preview as** to look at the portal as a client user.
