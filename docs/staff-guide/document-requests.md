Use a document request when you need a statement, receipt or anything else from the client. The client sees the list on their **Documents** page and uploads straight into it.

### Ask for a document

Either:

- click **+** in the top bar on any client page and choose **Request document**, or
- go to the client's **Client overview** and use the **Document requests** card.

Then fill in:

1. **Request** / **What you need**, for example *September bank statement*.
2. **Due** date (optional but recommended).
3. **Details (optional)**, for example *All pages, including the check images*.
4. Click **Request**. You'll see *Requested "September bank statement" from {client}.*

### Follow up on a request

On the **Document requests** card each request shows its due date, **Overdue · was due …**, or **Uploaded … · file**, plus who asked for it.

- **Open file**: view what the client uploaded.
- **Upload**: upload a file yourself (for example one they emailed you). Files are limited to 25 MB.
- Uploads go to the firm's Google Drive under *{client} / {year} / {document type}*. The type comes from the request's name (*September bank statement* goes to **Bank statements**), and the year from a year in the name, otherwise this year. See [Where are client files stored?](#/help/google-drive-files).
- **Mark received**: close the request once you have what you need.
- **Cancel**: withdraw a request you no longer need.

### Automatic reminder emails (Document reminders)

Under Document requests, the **Document reminders** card shows whether the client gets automatic reminders:

> On. Portal users get a reminder on day 0, day 3, day 7, then weekly until each document is uploaded.

- Click **Pause reminders** if you've agreed a later date with the client, or you're chasing them another way. Click **Resume reminders** to start again. Any staff member who works on the client can do this.
- Each open request shows how many reminders went out, or **no reminders yet**.
- **History** lists recent reminder emails and whether they were sent or skipped.

Reminders only go out when an admin has client emails turned on. See [How do I manage client emails?](#/help/client-emails).

### Troubleshooting

- **"This client is opted out of all emails" / "…of document reminders."** An admin can change that on the Emails page. The pause button is hidden while they're opted out.
- **"Test client, so reminders are never emailed."** Test clients never get emails. This is expected.
- **The card isn't there.** It's hidden when the client has no open requests, no reminder history and reminders aren't paused.
