Everyone on the team has a profile photo. Clients and teammates see it next to your name, and it appears in your email signature if you turn that on. We ask for one so the whole team looks the same everywhere clients see us.

### Add your photo

The first time you sign in (or the first time after this launched), a full-page **Add your profile photo** screen appears before anything else. You only do it once.

1. Click **Upload a photo** and pick a picture. On a phone or tablet, **Take a photo** opens your camera. On a computer with a webcam, **Take a photo** opens a small camera window with **Capture**.
2. Drag the picture to line up your face inside the circle. Use the slider (or your mouse wheel) to zoom.
3. Click **Use this photo**. You'll see **Thanks! Your photo is saved.**

A good photo is a clear, recent shot of your face and shoulders, looking at the camera in good light. No sunglasses, hats, filters or group photos. **Any background is fine**: an admin removes it later so every photo matches.

You can click **Settings** or **Sign out** on that screen at any time. If you're busy, click **Later** to keep working. The screen comes back every time you open the portal (or sign in again) until your photo is saved, so it can't be forgotten. If the upload fails, click **Try again**, or **Later**.

### What the photo needs

- A PNG, JPEG or WebP picture up to 15 MB. HEIC works only where your browser can open it.
- At least 400 × 400 pixels after you crop it.
- We remove location and camera details from the picture when it uploads.

### HEIC photos from an iPhone

Some browsers can't open HEIC. On a Mac, open the photo in Preview and choose **File › Export › JPEG**. On an iPhone, set **Settings › Camera › Formats** to **Most Compatible**, or take a screenshot of the photo, then try again.

### What an admin does to it

An admin downloads your photo, removes the background, and puts the edited version back. Your own photo stays safe in the history. You'll see the edited photo in the top bar, Settings, team lists and your email signature a little after you upload.

### Change it later

Open **Settings** (your initials at the top right, then **Settings**), find **Profile**, and click **Change photo**. You go through the same steps. Your new photo replaces the old one, and an admin edits the background again. You can't remove your photo; if you can't have one at all, ask an admin to mark it **Not required**.
