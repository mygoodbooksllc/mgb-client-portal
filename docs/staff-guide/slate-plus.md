**Slate plus** (October 28, 2026) is a batch of additions to Slate. Nothing you already use went away; a few things moved. This page lists each one and where to read more.

To see them on screen, click **?** in the top bar and pick **Slate plus tour** (about two minutes; it only looks and never changes a task).

### Finding things

- **One search.** The search box in the top bar finds clients, tasks, projects, people, SOPs and help in one place. Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) from any page. On the Slate page, the first result, **Filter this Slate list**, narrows your Slate to your words instead; it replaces the old search box next to **Add task**. See [Profiles, one search and Team load](#/help/slate-profiles).
- **An icon sidebar.** Slate's sidebar is a slim row of icons. Point at **People**, **Projects** or **Clients** for a quick menu, or click for the full panel with your pinned ones first. See [How do client task lists and projects work?](#/help/task-lists-and-projects).
- **Filters and saved filters.** A filter row on the Slate page (Assignee, Client, Project, Due, Status), with **Saved filters** and a default of your own. See [How do I use my Slate?](#/help/my-tasks).
- **Calendar.** **Calendar** above a list shows its tasks on a month grid by due date; drag a task to another day to move its due date. See [How do I use my Slate?](#/help/my-tasks).

### People

- **Profiles.** Click a person's name on a task row, in a task, in a comment, on Team or on a shout-out to see their week, their tasks and the clients they cover. The small button next to a name on a row reassigns the task.
- **Your own profile.** Add a status, your time zone, pinned clients and projects, goals for the quarter, About me, favorites and out of office, and choose the order of the sections.
- **Team load moved.** Open, due this week and late for each person, the Unassigned count and the next 4 weeks now live on **Team › People**, not on the Slate page.

All three are in [Profiles, one search and Team load](#/help/slate-profiles).

### Working a task

- **Blocked by.** Make a task wait for another one. See [How does Blocked by work?](#/help/task-dependencies).
- **Custom fields.** A project or client list can have its own fields, such as Fund or Grant. See [How do custom fields work?](#/help/custom-fields).
- **Clearer @mentions.** The **@** list grays out anyone who can't see the task, and a line after you post says who was notified. See [What's in a task's details?](#/help/task-details).
- **Hours against budget.** On a client with a monthly hours budget, Slate shows this month's hours against it next to the client's name and at the top of the **Assign** menu. See [How do client task lists and projects work?](#/help/task-lists-and-projects).

### Projects

- **New project** takes a description, a client, a due date, starter sections and people in one go.
- **Project status updates** (On track, At risk, Off track, Done) on each project, and **Portfolio**, one table of every project's latest status and due date.
- **Client tag.** A project tied to a client shows a **Client** tag next to its name.

See [How do client task lists and projects work?](#/help/task-lists-and-projects).

### For admins

- **Rules** do small repeat steps on one project or one client. Every rule starts off, and only the owner switches one on. See [How do Slate rules work?](#/help/slate-rules).
- **Teams and managers.** Admins make teams on **Team › People** and set each person's manager in **HR › Employee records**; both show on profiles.

### Where it isn't

Slate plus is staff only. It's not in **View as** (an admin viewing as a bookkeeper sees Slate without it), and clients never see any of it.
