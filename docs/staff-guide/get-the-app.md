You can put MyGoodBooks on your phone's home screen or your computer's app list. It opens in its own window with no browser bar, like any other app. It's the same portal, and only staff see the install option for now.

### iPhone or iPad

1. Open **app.mygoodbooks.org** in **Safari** and sign in.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open MyGoodBooks from the new icon on your home screen.

The home-screen app keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari. Sign in again inside it the first time. See **Signing in to the installed app** below.

### Android

1. Open **app.mygoodbooks.org** in **Chrome** and sign in.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Tap **Install**. Open MyGoodBooks from your home screen or app drawer.

### Computer (Chrome or Edge)

1. Open **app.mygoodbooks.org** and sign in.
2. Click the **install** icon at the right end of the address bar (a small screen with a down arrow), then **Install**. If you don't see it, open the **⋮** (Chrome) or **…** (Edge) menu and choose **Install MyGoodBooks** (Chrome: *Cast, save and share → Install page as app*).
3. The app opens in its own window and shows up in your Start menu or Applications folder.

Safari on a Mac can add it too: **File → Add to Dock**.

### Signing in to the installed app

Sign in the same way as in the browser: on the first screen use the small **MyGoodBooks staff? Sign in with Google** link at the bottom. If Google won't finish signing in inside the app (this can happen on iPhone and iPad):

1. On the first screen, type your **@mygoodbooks.org email** in the box and tap **Email me a sign-in link**.
2. Open that email, and **type the code** into the app's **Sign-in code** box. Don't tap the link: it opens Safari, not the app.
3. Tap **Sign in**. The app takes you to the staff portal.

The code only works if you have signed in to the portal with Google at least once (do that first in Safari). You stay signed in until you sign out or the sign-in expires.

### Troubleshooting

- **I don't see an install option.** Sign in first. The option only appears for signed-in staff. On iPhone or iPad use Safari (other browsers may not show it).
- **The installed app asks me to sign in again.** The installed app keeps its own sign-in. Sign in once inside it.
- **I want to remove it.** Press and hold the icon and choose **Remove App** (iPhone), or right-click it and uninstall (computer).
