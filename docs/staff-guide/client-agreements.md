Every client has an **Agreements** card on their **Documents** tab. It keeps the signed service agreement, engagement letter and any changes in one place, with the dates that matter.

### Who can do what

- **Admins** and **the client's account manager** add, edit, mark signed, show or hide, and move agreements to the trash.
- **Bookkeepers** who work on the client can read the card, open the files and see the staff notes. They can't change anything.
- **Restoring from the trash** is admin only. Nothing is ever deleted for good.
- While you're using **View as**, the card is read only.

### What each agreement shows

- **Title** and **type**: Service agreement, Engagement letter, Addendum, NDA, Pricing change or Other.
- **Status**: Draft, Sent, Signed, Expired or Terminated. A signed agreement whose renewal date has passed shows **Expired**.
- **Dates**: signed, effective, and an optional **Renews or expires** date. When that date is within 60 days, an **Expires in N days** pill shows.
- **Visible to client** or **Staff only**. New agreements start **Staff only**.
- **Open**: the Google Drive link, or a file already in the client's Documents.
- **Staff note**: for the team only. It's never sent to the client, even when the agreement is visible.

### Add an agreement

1. Open the client, then the **Documents** tab.
2. On the **Agreements** card, click **Add agreement**.
3. Fill in the title, type, status and dates.
4. Paste the **Link** (it must start with https://, for example a Google Drive share link), or pick **a file already in Documents**.
5. Add a **Staff note** if it helps, and turn on **Visible to client** if the client should see it.
6. Click **Add agreement**.

If you attach a file that is **Staff only** in Documents and make the agreement visible, the card warns you: the client wouldn't be able to open that file. Share the file first, or use a link.

### Add old signed documents in one go

Click **Paste several links** and paste one per line:

- `Service agreement 2023 | https://drive.google.com/...`
- two spreadsheet columns (title, then link), or
- just the link (it's titled *Signed agreement*).

Pick one **type** and **status** for the batch (it starts on Service agreement, Signed), then click **Add**. Lines without an https:// link are skipped and listed. Everything pasted starts **Staff only**; check each one, then click **Show to client**.

### Row actions

- **Mark signed**: sets the status to Signed, and the signed date to today if it was blank.
- **Show to client** / **Hide from client**.
- **Edit**.
- **Move to trash**: the agreement leaves the list. **Show trash** lists it, and an admin can click **Restore**.

### What clients see

**Nothing yet.** Client agreements are off for the whole firm until the owner turns them on, and the card says so. Once they're on, a client sees an **Agreements** card on their Documents page with the agreements marked **Visible to client**: title, type, status, signed and renewal dates, and **Open**. They can't edit anything, can't sign, and never see the staff note or staff-only agreements.

### Signing in the portal

Not available yet. Admins see a grayed-out **Client signing: not available yet** box on the form. It's a placeholder for a future feature; for now, collect signatures the usual way and add the signed copy here.
