Across the top of every staff page there's a dark bar, joined to the rail, with your most-used tools. Clients never see it, and it goes away while you use **Preview as** or **View as client**. The rail on the left has the five places (Today, Inbox, Work, Clients, Team); the bar has no page links of its own.

### Left side

- **Back**, **Forward** and **Refresh** (installed app only): when you use MyGoodBooks as an installed app, there's no browser toolbar, so these three round buttons come first. **Back** and **Forward** walk the pages you have opened (each is grayed out when there's nowhere to go), and **Refresh** reloads the page. The Slate app has the same three beside its logo. In a browser tab, use the browser's own buttons.
- **Client picker**: shows the client you have open, with their plan and health (for example "Pro · Looking good"). Click it to search for and open another client. On a staff page it says **Choose a client**. More in [How do I find and open a client?](#/help/finding-a-client).
- **Sync pill** (client pages only): how often the client's plan syncs, and when it last did: **Every hour · synced 5m ago** (Pro) or **Synced monthly · synced …** (Basic). Click it to run **Sync now** for that client. Clients without QuickBooks show **Setting up — connecting QuickBooks** instead (test clients show **Prototype · Sample Data**). See [How do I connect a client's QuickBooks and use Sync now?](#/help/quickbooks-connection).

### Search (middle)

Click the search box (**Search people, clients, tasks, help**), or press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) from anywhere. With nothing typed it lists every **page** and **action** you can jump to (Work › Close, HR › Reviews, **Send feedback**, **Settings** and so on). Type a few letters and the results are grouped:

- **Filter this Slate list** (only on the Slate page): narrows your Slate to your words instead of opening anything. See [How do I use my Slate?](#/help/my-tasks).
- **People**: teammates by name. Picking one opens their profile. See [Profiles, one search and Team load](#/help/slate-profiles).
- **Clients**: opens the client on the tab you last used for them, or on the Client overview if you haven't been there yet this session.
- **Projects**: projects you can see.
- **Tasks**: tasks you can see whose name, description or comments match. A match inside a comment says *In a comment:*. Picking one opens the task's details. **See all task matches** opens the full list.
- **Notes**: your notes that match.
- **SOPs**: client SOPs that mention your words. Opens that client's **SOP** tab.
- **Help**: articles from this guide.
- **Pages** and **Actions**: anywhere in the portal, by name.

The open client's transactions, budget categories, documents and messages aren't in this search. Open that client's tab to find them.

Use the **↑** and **↓** keys to move through the results and **Enter** to open one. **Esc** clears the box, and a second **Esc** closes it.

### Right side

- **QuickBooks syncs slowed** / **QuickBooks syncs stopped** (admins only): appears only when the firm is close to Intuit's monthly limit. Click it to open **Team › Hours**, which has the details.
- **Bell** (Notifications): a red number shows how many new items you haven't looked at. It lists client messages waiting for a reply, documents a client has uploaded, tasks a teammate assigned to you, **mentioned you** when someone @mentions you in a task comment, shout-outs for you, and clients whose last month-end close is **Blocked**. You can turn each kind off in **Settings → Notifications**. Click an item to go straight to it: a message opens that message (right there on the Inbox if you're on it, otherwise on the client's **Messages** tab), an upload opens **Documents** at the request, a task or a mention opens the task's details, and a blocked close opens the client's **Close** tab at the QuickBooks check. Opening the bell marks everything as seen (in this browser).
- **Slate**: the number of your open tasks. A red number next to it counts the overdue ones. Click it to open the Slate page at **Slate**, or, with a client open, that client's task panel. See [How do I see a client's tasks without leaving the page?](#/help/client-tasks-drawer). With the Desk on it is a plain **Slate** link with no numbers (the Desk's bar shows what's late and due today); see [How do I use the Desk?](#/help/workbench).
- **+**: with a client open, **New task**, **New note**, **Request document** or **Message** for that client. On a staff page with no client open it says **Add** and has **New task**. See [How do I add a task, note or document request, or message a client?](#/help/quick-actions). Pressing **q** on any staff page also adds a task. With the Desk on, on a computer, this **+** moves to the foot of the Desk's rail on the right (same menu); on a phone it stays in the top bar.
- **?** (Help): **Help for this page** (the article for where you are), **Staff guide** (this guide), **Send feedback**, **What's new**, **Tips while you learn: On** / **Off** (click it to flip the switch for the first-30-days tips; see [How do tips work?](#/help/tips)) and the tours: **Bookkeeper tour** for everyone, plus **Admin tour** for admins, **Temporary admin tour** while you have temporary admin access, **Account manager tour** for account managers, **Slate tour** and **Slate, start to finish** (seven short chapters; see [How do I take the Slate tour?](#/help/slate-tour)) for everyone. On a Slate page on a computer, **Slate settings** opens the Slate app on its Settings page (see [How do I change my Slate settings?](#/help/slate-settings)). After each launch there is also a **New: … tour** line you can run again any time (see [What's new](#/help/whats-new)). See [How do I report a bug or send feedback?](#/help/send-feedback).
- **Your initials or photo** (account menu):
  - your name and role
  - **Temporary admin access · expires …** or **Temporary access: {client} · …** when you have short-term access
  - **Dark mode** / **Light mode**
  - **Preview as a client user** (client pages only): pick a person to see the portal the way they do. See [How do I see the portal the way a client sees it?](#/help/preview-as).
  - **Preview plan** (client pages only, not during View as): **Actual plan (…)**, **Basic** or **Pro**. A check marks the one showing.
  - **Exit "View as"** when an admin is using View as (the bar says it is read-only: nothing you do there is saved)
  - **Settings**: your profile, notifications, theme, start page and signature. See [How do I change my settings?](#/help/staff-settings).
  - **Sign out**

Press **Esc** to close any open menu.

### On a phone

The bar keeps only the client picker, search, the bell, **+** and **?**. The sync pill stays in the page header. Everything else (the five places, **Settings**, **Sign out**, **Preview as** and **Preview plan**) is in the menu: tap the menu button at the top left. The **?** menu works the same as on a computer: help for this page, the staff guide, feedback, what's new, tips and the tours.

### In a narrow window

On a computer, when the window is narrow the bar makes room instead of squeezing things together:

- The **Ctrl K** / **⌘K** hint inside the search box hides first. The shortcut still works.
- Search shrinks to a round magnifier button whenever there isn't room for a usable box. Click it, or press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K**), and it opens across the bar.
- Below about 900px wide the sync pill moves to the page header (click it there to Sync now). In a wider but crowded bar it trims its wording with "…".
- The client picker shows just the client's initials. Click it to switch clients as usual.
- **Slate** hides from the bar. It's still under **Work** in the rail. Press **t** for the client's task panel.
- In the tightest case **+** hides too. **?** never hides, so help, feedback and the tours stay one click away.

Widen the window and everything comes back.

### Troubleshooting

- **The bell or Slate button is missing.** They're hidden while an admin is using **View as**, because they'd show the admin's own items.
- **The + button is missing.** It's hidden in the tightest narrow window, and while you use **Preview as**. Press **q** to add a task instead.
- **Search says "No matches".** Search looks at page and action names, people, your clients, projects, tasks you can see, your notes, SOP text and help articles. Try fewer letters, or part of a word (*recon* finds *Reconcile*).
- **The bell number came back after I cleared it.** Something new arrived, or you're on a different browser or computer (what you've seen is remembered per browser).
