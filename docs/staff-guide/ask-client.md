When a task can't move until the client tells you something, sends a file or says yes, ask them from the task. The task waits on the client until they answer, and you see every open ask in one place.

### Ask from a task

1. Open a client task. **Client requests** sits above **Files and links** in the task details.
2. Click **Ask client**. It's there for a client task in **To do**, **In progress** or **Waiting on client**. A task in review or done says why it can't ask (take it back from review, or reopen it, first).
3. Pick what you need: **Question** (they type an answer), **Document** (they upload a file) or **Approval** (they say yes or no).
4. Add a short **Title** and a **Message** in plain words. They see both. For a question or approval you can tick **They should upload a file too**. **Needed by** is optional.
5. Under **Who should answer?**, tick one or more people: the client's portal users, or its **contacts** (people without a portal sign-in, like a treasurer, who answer through a link in the email). Up to 20 people.
6. Click **Ask**. The task moves to **Waiting on client**.

**Client emails for requests are a dry run for now.** Nothing is emailed. The toast says *Dry run: nothing sent* and who it would have gone to. The owner switches real emails on later; until then, tell the client yourself and use **Record answer** when they reply.

### Add a client contact

In the Ask client window, click **+ Add contact**, then enter a name, an email and a role (Treasurer, Pastor, Board, Finance, Other) and click **Save contact**. It's ticked straight away. A contact belongs to that client only, and the same email can't be added twice. Contacts can't sign in to the portal; they only answer the request they were sent.

### Follow up

Each request in **Client requests** shows its status (**Waiting**, **No reply**, **Answered**, **Accepted**, **Cancelled**), who was asked, how many business days it has waited, how many reminders went out, and the answer once there is one.

- **Email again** sends the request again with a fresh link, and the link from the earlier email stops working.
- **Send reminder** sends a short reminder. Links already sent keep working.
- **Pause reminders** / **Resume reminders** stops or restarts automatic reminders for that request.
- **Turn off links** stops every answer link for that request (for example if it went to the wrong person). They can still answer in the portal.
- **Record answer** is for an answer that came by phone or email. It closes the request and adds the answer to the task's comments.
- **Cancel** (with an optional reason) withdraws a request you no longer need. Its links stop working.

After four reminders with no answer the chip says **No reply**. Call them.

### When they answer

The answer shows on the request and in the task's comments, marked *(client)* or *(by link)*. When the last open request on a task is answered or cancelled, the task goes back to where it was before. Then:

- **Accept** if the answer is what you needed.
- **Ask again** opens a new request with the same details if it isn't.

While a task has open requests it stays in **Waiting on client**. To move it on, record their answer or cancel the request.

### Waiting on client

**Tasks › Waiting on client** (the hourglass in the Tasks sidebar, `#/tasks/waiting`) lists every open request for the clients you work on, grouped by client with the longest wait first. **Just my tasks** shows only tasks you own. Click a request to open its task, or use **Send reminder**, **Email again** or **Cancel** right there.

### Document requests on the Documents tab

Document requests made from a client's **Documents** tab aren't linked to a task, so they don't show in **Client requests** or **Waiting on client**. Manage them on the Documents tab as before. See [How do I request a document from a client?](#/help/document-requests).
