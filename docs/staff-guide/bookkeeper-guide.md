As a bookkeeper you look after your own clients: you keep their books, answer their messages and close each month. This is the written version of the **Bookkeeper tour**, with a routine to follow.

### Take the tour

- It starts by itself once, the first time you open the portal. If you did the older staff tour, you still get this one, because it's new.
- Run it again any time from **?** → **Bookkeeper tour**, from **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) by typing "bookkeeper tour", or from **Settings › Help**.
- It opens each part of your day in turn and points at it: **Today**, **Clients**, a client's **Overview**, the **+** button, the **Inbox**, **Work › Tasks** and **Close** (which points you to **Deadlines**), **Team › People** and **Reviews**, search and the **?** menu. About three minutes.
- Admins can run it from the **?** menu too. During **View as**, it only visits the pages View as can open (Today, Clients and a client's pages); the Inbox, Work and Team stops are skipped.

If you're also a client's account manager, the **Account manager tour** comes next. If you have temporary admin access, you'll get the **Temporary admin tour** first. See [Account manager guide](#/help/account-manager-guide) and [Temporary access](#/help/temporary-access).

### 1. Today

**Today** is where you start. One list of what needs you across all your clients, most urgent first: client messages waiting for a reply, tasks due, closes that are late or blocked, filings coming up. Click a row to go straight to it. See [What's on my Today page?](#/help/home-page).

### 2. Your clients and the client Overview

1. Open **Clients**. It starts on **Mine**, the clients you're assigned to. **All** and **Needs attention** are one click away.
2. Each row shows **Health** and **Hours this month** at a glance. The hours come from QuickBooks Time. There's nothing to log in the portal. See [QuickBooks Time and in-app time](#/help/quickbooks-time-and-app-time) and [How do I set a monthly hours budget for a client?](#/help/hours-budget).
3. Click a client to open it. You land on the **Overview**, the page only staff see. It has the month-end close checklist, document requests, key dates and coverage, and what the client has been sent.
4. The **SOP** tab says how this client's books are done. Read it before you start a new month, and fix it when it's out of date.

See [How do I use the Clients page?](#/help/clients-page), [What does the Client overview show?](#/help/client-overview) and [How do I write or update a client's SOP?](#/help/client-sops).

### 3. The + button

With a client open, **+** in the top bar adds something for that client without leaving the page: **New task**, **New note**, **Request document** or **Message**. A document request shows on the client's **Documents** page until they upload it.

See [How do I add a task, note or document request, or message a client?](#/help/quick-actions) and [How do I request a document from a client?](#/help/document-requests).

### 4. Inbox

1. Client messages land in the **Inbox**. **Waiting on you** means the client is waiting for a reply.
2. Open the conversation. **Reply** sends to the client. **Note** stays inside the team, and the client never sees it.
3. Aim to reply within **24 hours**, nights and weekends included.

See [How do I message a client or a teammate?](#/help/inbox).

### 5. Work

- **Tasks**: your own tasks, plus your **Notes** and **SOPs** cards. See [How do I add and manage my tasks?](#/help/my-tasks).
- **Close**: month-end close, one row per client and one column per month. Click a cell, choose **Done** (or another **Status**) and **Save**. The QuickBooks badges tell you what's still blocking a month. See [How do I track month-end close?](#/help/month-end-close).
- **Deadlines**: 990s, 1099s and the other filing dates for your clients, with **Mark filed** and **Create task**. See [How do I track filing deadlines?](#/help/deadlines).

### 6. Team

- **People**: who's out and who's covering. Add your own time off with **+ Add time off**, and make sure each of your clients has a backup. See [How do I add time off and make sure my clients are covered?](#/help/time-off-coverage).
- **Reviews**: your quarterly review. When a cycle opens, fill in your self-review. Then meet your account manager and you both sign. See [How do quarterly reviews work?](#/help/quarterly-reviews).
- **Performance**: your score out of 100 and what's behind it. You see your own, never the ranking. See [How does Team › Performance score bookkeepers?](#/help/performance).

### 7. Search and help

- **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) finds a client, a task or an SOP, and jumps to any page. See [What's on the bar at the top of the page?](#/help/top-bar).
- **?** has help for the page you're on, this guide, **Send feedback**, **What's new** and the tours.

### A routine

**Every day**

1. **Today**: clear what's marked **Now**.
2. **Inbox**: reply to every client waiting on you.
3. **Work › Tasks**: finish what's due.

**Every week**

1. **Clients › Needs attention**: anything at risk, late or stale?
2. **Hours this month** on **Clients**: any client close to its hours budget?
3. **Work › Deadlines**: what's due in the next week or two?

**Every month**

1. **Work › Close**: take each client's month to **Done**, and clear any **Blocked** or **Behind** badge first.
2. Update the client's SOP if the way you did the books changed.

**Every quarter**

1. Fill in your self-review on **Team › Reviews**, then meet your account manager and sign.

**When you're away**

1. Add your time off on **Team › People** and set each client's backup on the **Overview** (**Key dates and coverage** › **Edit dates and coverage**).

### Good to know

- You only see the clients assigned to you. To cover someone else's, [request temporary access](#/help/temporary-access).
- Hours aren't logged here. QuickBooks Time is where hours come from, and in-app time is automatic and not billed.
- If a client can't see something, **View as client** shows the portal exactly as they see it. See [How do I see the portal the way a client sees it?](#/help/preview-as).
- New here? Your checklist is on **Team › Onboarding**. See [How does the new-hire onboarding checklist work?](#/help/new-hire-onboarding).
- Stuck? **?** → **Help for this page**, or search this guide.
