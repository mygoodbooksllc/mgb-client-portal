The **account manager** is a client's main contact. Every client has one, and a new client starts with Jesse. The client sees you on their Home as **Your account manager** (or **Your account manager and bookkeeper** when you're both), and you get the email when they send a message.

The account manager also does every bookkeeper's quarterly review. This is the written version of the **Account manager tour**, with a routine to follow.

### Take the tour

- It shows for anyone who is the account manager on at least one client. It starts by itself once, after your other pending tours (Temporary admin, Bookkeeper or Admin), and runs again from **?** → **Account manager tour**, **Ctrl+K** / **⌘K**, or **Settings › Help**.
- It covers the five parts below. Stops on admin-only pages are left out if you're not an admin.

### 1. Bookkeeper reviews

You review every bookkeeper, every quarter.

1. An admin opens the cycle on **Team › Reviews › Team status** and you're picked as the reviewer (you're the **Default reviewer**). You get an email.
2. Open **Team › Reviews › Reviews I'm giving** and fill in a review for each person: **Camaraderie**, **Ownership** and **Healthy Hustle**, two items each, rated 1 to 5. The **Appreciation** box shows their shout-outs from the quarter.
3. Anything below 3 needs a comment and an action step. Your scores and theirs stay hidden until both forms are in.
4. Meet, agree on action steps (owner, due date, status), and both sign. The review then locks and its PDF is saved to the Staff Reviews Shared Drive.

See [How do quarterly reviews work?](#/help/quarterly-reviews) and [How do I run a review cycle?](#/help/review-cycles).

### 2. Client messages

1. When one of your clients writes, you get an email. Open **Inbox**.
2. Reply there, in the portal, so the client sees it in their **Messages** and it counts toward reply times. The goal is a reply within **24 hours**, nights and weekends included.
3. Expect payroll questions: clients are told to message their account manager for payroll pricing.
4. Admins can watch **Team › Reply times** to see how fast each person answers.

See [How do I message a client or a teammate?](#/help/inbox) and [How do I read reply times?](#/help/reply-times).

### 3. New clients

Follow [New client, start to finish](#/help/new-client-start-to-finish). In short:

1. **Settings › Firm settings › Client roster** → add the organization: name, type, plan, payroll add-on, bookkeeper and account manager.
2. Add the client's portal logins.
3. Connect their QuickBooks, then let the first sync and onboarding run.
4. On their **Client overview**, set the monthly hours budget and confirm the pricing milestone. See [Hours budget](#/help/hours-budget) and [Pricing milestones](#/help/pricing-milestones).

### 4. Client health check-ins

1. **Clients** → **Needs attention** picks out clients with health at risk, a late close, a portal access request waiting, or a stale SOP.
2. The **Health** and **Hours this month** columns show the rest at a glance. Amber hours means 80% of the budget is used; red means over.
3. Open a client: the **Client health** card on the Overview says why points were taken off, and **Key dates and coverage** has their 990 and 1099 dates, board meetings and fiscal year end.
4. Clients at risk also show on **Today**.

See [What does client health mean?](#/help/client-health) and [How do I use the Clients page?](#/help/clients-page).

### 5. Plans and upgrades

1. An **upgrade** or **Add Payroll** request from a client's Plan tab shows on **Today**. Follow up with them, then mark it **Contacted**, **Completed** or **Dismiss**.
2. When a client signs up for payroll, tick **Payroll add-on** on their row in **Client roster**. Their Payroll tab appears straight away.
3. Fees follow the confirmed **pricing milestone**. On Today, turn on **Also show › Milestones** to see clients whose numbers point to a different milestone.
4. Admins: **Team › Hours** shows each client's hours against budget; click a client for fees, cost and margin.
5. To see what a client would get on another plan, use **Preview plan**. See [Preview as a client](#/help/preview-as).

See [Staff and access](#/help/staff-management) (payroll add-on), [Pricing milestones](#/help/pricing-milestones) and [Profitability](#/help/profitability).

### A routine

- **Every day**: Inbox, then the requests and at-risk clients on Today.
- **Every week**: **Clients › Needs attention**, and reply times if you're an admin.
- **Every month**: hours against budget, pricing milestones to confirm, and whether each client's close is done.
- **Every quarter**: the bookkeeper reviews.
