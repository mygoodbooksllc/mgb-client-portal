**Work › Deadlines** (`#/work/deadlines`) lists the filing deadlines for every client you can see.

**Always verify a date before you file.** The dates are a starting point the firm keeps up to date, not tax or legal advice. When a due date lands on a weekend it moves to Monday. Holidays aren't handled, so check those yourself.

### Find what's due

- **List** shows **Overdue and not filed** (the last 6 months), **Next 7 days** and **Later (next 90 days)**.
- **Month** shows a calendar. Click a day to see just that day. Red means overdue, amber means due within a week.
- **My clients** / **All clients** switches between your assigned clients and everyone you can see.
- **All forms** narrows the list to one form. Tick **Show filed** to see what's done.

### Mark it filed

Click **Mark filed**, add a note if you like (for example "e-filed, confirmation 1234"), then **Save**. The whole team sees who filed it and when. Made a mistake? Click **Undo**.

**Create task** adds the deadline to **Work › Tasks** with the due date.

### Which deadlines a client gets

Each deadline has rules: nonprofits or for-profits, payroll clients only, or organization types to leave out (the Form 990 leaves out churches). Some deadlines are **Off by default** and only show for clients you turn them on for.

The Form 990 is counted from the client's **fiscal year end** (set on the client's **Key dates**). If none is set, May 15 is assumed. The **Form 990** and **1099** due dates on **Key dates** also count: they replace the worked-out date.

### Client exceptions

Under **Client exceptions**, pick a client and a deadline, then choose **Skip for this client**, **Different date** (yearly deadlines only) or **Turn on for this client**, and **Save**. Click **Remove** to undo an exception.

### Deadline rules (admins)

Admins see **+ Add deadline**, **Edit** and **Turn off** under **Deadline rules**. Pick how often (every year, quarter or month) and the due date. Say "verify" in the description. Deadlines are never deleted: **Turn off** hides one and **Turn on** brings it back.

Deadlines coming up also appear as rows in Today's **Needs you** list, with anything overdue and not filed under **Now**.
