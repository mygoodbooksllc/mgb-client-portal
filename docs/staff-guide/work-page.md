**Work** (in the left rail) is where your own work lives. It has four tabs. The tab is part of the address, so a link such as `#/work/close` opens straight to it:

- **Tasks** (`#/work/tasks`): **My Tasks**, everything assigned to you, grouped as Overdue, Today, This week, Later, Waiting on client and Done. See [How do I add and manage my tasks?](#/help/my-tasks). For client lists and projects, open the full Tasks page from the **Tasks** button in the top bar; see [How do client task lists and projects work?](#/help/task-lists-and-projects).
- **Notes & SOPs** (`#/work/notes`): the **Notes** and **SOPs** cards that used to sit under Tasks, working as before, plus **Handoff summaries**, one short summary per client. See [How do I keep notes on a client?](#/help/notes) and [How do I write or update a client's SOP?](#/help/client-sops).
- **Close** (`#/work/close`): every client's month-end close, month by month, with the automatic QuickBooks checks. See [How do I track month-end close?](#/help/month-end-close).
- **Deadlines** (`#/work/deadlines`): filing deadlines for every client you can see, with **Mark filed** and **Create task**. See [How do I track filing deadlines?](#/help/deadlines).

Bookkeepers see their own clients. Admins see everyone's.

### Getting there fast

- Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) and type "close" or "deadlines" to jump to a tab.
- Press **q** on any staff page to add a task.
- The number on the **Tasks** button in the top bar is your open tasks, with overdue ones in red. Click it to open the Tasks page.
- Today's **Tasks due today**, **Overdue** and **Closes open** tiles open the matching tab.
- If you'd rather land on your task list than on Today when you sign in, pick **Work › Tasks** under **Appearance & start page** in [Settings](#/help/staff-settings).
