**Work** (in the left rail) is where your own work lives. It has three tabs. The tab is part of the address, so a link such as `#/work/close` opens straight to it:

- **Tasks** (`#/work/tasks`): your to-do list, with the **Today**, **Upcoming**, **Overdue**, **All** and **By client** views, and the **Notes** and **SOPs** cards below it. See [How do I add and manage my tasks?](#/help/my-tasks), [How do I keep notes on a client?](#/help/notes) and [How do I write or update a client's SOP?](#/help/client-sops).
- **Close** (`#/work/close`): every client's month-end close, month by month, with the automatic QuickBooks checks. See [How do I track month-end close?](#/help/month-end-close).
- **Deadlines** (`#/work/deadlines`): filing deadlines for every client you can see, with **Mark filed** and **Create task**. See [How do I track filing deadlines?](#/help/deadlines).

Bookkeepers see their own clients. Admins see everyone's.

### Getting there fast

- Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) and type "close" or "deadlines" to jump to a tab.
- The number on the **Tasks** button in the top bar is your open tasks, with overdue ones in red. Click it to open **Work › Tasks**.
- Today's **Tasks due today**, **Overdue** and **Closes open** tiles open the matching tab.
- If you'd rather land on your task list than on Today when you sign in, pick **Work › Tasks** under **Appearance & start page** in [Settings](#/help/staff-settings).
