**Work** (in the left rail) is where your own work lives. It has five tabs. The tab is part of the address, so a link such as `#/work/close` opens straight to it:

- **Slate** (`#/work/tasks`): a short summary of your Slate: what's **Late**, what's **Due today**, and your **Next** five tasks, with an **Open Slate** button to the full Slate page. Click a row to open the task, the same way as on the Slate page. The Slate page is the one full Slate, with the groups, filters, right-click, drag, select-many and swipes. See [How do I use my Slate?](#/help/my-tasks). For client lists and projects, use the Slate page too; see [How do client task lists and projects work?](#/help/task-lists-and-projects). If you're on the **Classic tasks page**, or the Slate app is switched off, this tab still shows the full list as before.
- **Notes & SOPs** (`#/work/notes`): the **Notes** and **SOPs** cards that used to sit under Slate, working as before, plus **Handoff summaries**, one short summary per client. See [How do I keep notes on a client?](#/help/notes) and [How do I write or update a client's SOP?](#/help/client-sops).
- **Close** (`#/work/close`): every client's month-end close, month by month, with the automatic QuickBooks checks. See [How do I track month-end close?](#/help/month-end-close).
- **Ledger review** (`#/work/ledger`): **Review a file**. Drop in an exported General Ledger and it's checked in your browser; nothing is saved. See [How does Ledger review work?](#/help/ledger-review).
- **Deadlines** (`#/work/deadlines`): filing deadlines for every client you can see, with **Mark filed** and **Create task**. See [How do I track filing deadlines?](#/help/deadlines).

Bookkeepers see their own clients. Admins see everyone's.

In **View as** (admins), Work shows the bookkeeper's tabs read-only. **Slate** is a read-only snapshot of their tasks with private tasks hidden, and nothing on it can be changed or popped out. (It is the full snapshot, not the short summary.)

### Getting there fast

- Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) and type "close" or "deadlines" to jump to a tab.
- Press **q** on any staff page to add a task.
- The number on the **Slate** button in the top bar is your open tasks, with overdue ones in red. Click it to open the Slate page.
- Work › Slate's **Open Slate** button, Today's **Open Slate** link and its **Do next** card open Slate; closes that are late or blocked show under **Clients needing attention** and **Also needs you**.
- If you'd rather land on your task list than on Today when you sign in, pick **Work › Slate** under **Appearance & start page** in [Settings](#/help/staff-settings).
