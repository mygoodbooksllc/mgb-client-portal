Once a quarter you fill in a short self-review, your reviewer (usually Jesse) fills in the same form about you, and then you meet, agree on action steps and both sign. Everything lives on the **Reviews** tab of **Team** (`#/team/reviews`). The number on the tab shows how many things are waiting on you.

When a review is due, you also get an email, a **Review due** row on **Today**, and a number on the **Reviews** tab of **Team**.

### The form

Three sections, two items each, rated 1 to 5 (30 points in all):

- **Camaraderie**: how you support the team, and the care you put into clients.
- **Ownership**: owning your tools, deadlines and clients, and working efficiently.
- **Healthy Hustle**: work-life balance, and managing allowable hours per client.

The scale is the same on every item: **1** Not meeting expectations, **2** Partly meeting, **3** Meeting, **4** Often exceeding, **5** Consistently exceeding.

Rules the form checks before you can submit:

- Every item needs a rating.
- Any rating below 3 needs a comment in that section, and an action step.
- A total below 18 needs comments in all three sections and an action step.

On the reviewer's form, the **Appreciation** box shows that person's shout-outs from the quarter, for reference only. They aren't part of the review. See [How do I give a teammate a shout-out?](#/help/shoutouts).

Your answers save as a draft while you work. Once you submit, the form is read-only.

### Blind until both are in

You can't see your reviewer's scores, and they can't see yours, until **both** forms are submitted. This applies to admins too.

### Compare, meet, sign

Once both forms are in, **My review** shows the comparison: your score, your reviewer's score, the gap, and a flag for each item (**Action step required**, **Discuss** for a gap of 2 or more, or **Aligned**).

After your meeting, the agreed action steps are added with an owner, a due date and a status. Steps that aren't done carry forward to next quarter's review, marked "From Q3 2026" (or whichever quarter they came from).

To sign, type your full name and check the box. You can sign before or after your reviewer. If you disagree with parts of the review you can still sign: check **I disagree with parts of this review** and say why. Your note is saved with your signature and printed on the PDF.

When both of you have signed, the review locks for good. Nothing in it can be edited after that. If something needs correcting, an admin adds an addendum.

### History and PDFs

**History** shows your reviews by year: total score by quarter (yours and your reviewer's), your year average, how close your ratings were to your reviewer's, and how many action steps you've completed. Click **Download PDF** next to any signed review to get your copy. Once an admin confirms your year-end summary, it shows at the bottom of History with its own **Download PDF** button.

### Good to know

- Reviews are kept permanently. They're never deleted.
- Admins can see every review. Other staff can't see yours.
- If a review isn't signed by the time the quarter's cycle closes, it's marked **Closed unsigned** and kept, read-only.
