Add your **birthday** and **start date** in **Settings › Profile** so the team can celebrate with you. Teammates see the day and month; only admins see the birth year.

### Your first sign-in

The first time you sign in, Today asks you to finish your profile. **Later** closes the prompt, and a **Complete your profile** item stays on your Today list until both dates are filled in.

### Where celebrations show

- **Team › People › Celebrations** lists everyone's birthdays and work anniversaries in the next 30 days.
- **Today (admins)** shows each one a week ahead with three buttons. **Send shout-out** opens the shout-out composer with the teammate already picked, **Gift sent** records that a gift went out, and **Dismiss** hides it for this year. A birthday that is today sits in "needs you now".
- **Weekly digest (admins)** gets a "Coming up" section with the next two weeks.

### Admins: filling in dates for someone

**Team › Members** has a **Birthday** and a **Start date** column. Type or pick a date and it saves on its own. Staff can change their own dates in Settings › Profile at any time.
