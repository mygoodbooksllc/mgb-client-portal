Click a task anywhere (Slate, a client or project list, Today, a client's Overview, the bell or search) and its details slide in from the right. On a phone they fill the screen. Press **Esc** or click **Close** to put them away. Changes save as you make them.

With the Desk on, the details open inside the Desk column instead, beside the page, with **← Back** at the top. **Esc** or Back goes from a subtask to its parent, then to the list; **D** closes the Desk and the task waits there for 5 minutes. Clicking the open task's row again closes its task view. See [How do I use the Desk?](#/help/workbench).

### The header

- **Title**: click to edit.
- **Copy link**: copies a link that opens this task after sign-in. You'll see *Link copied.* Links in older emails keep working too.
- **More actions**: **Save as template** (admins) or **Suggest as template** (everyone else), and **Delete**. See [How do I use my Slate?](#/help/my-tasks).

### Status and review

Every task has one of five statuses: **To do**, **In progress**, **Waiting on client**, **Ready for review** and **Done**.

- **Waiting on client** pauses the task. It moves to the Waiting on client group on Slate and never shows as overdue. **Ask client** (below) sets it for you, and while a request is open the task stays waiting until the client answers or you cancel the request.
- A task with **Needs review before it's done** ticked has a review step, for admins too. When you finish it, the status menu says **Send for review** and the task goes to **Ready for review**. You'll see *Sent for review. An admin will approve it.* A strip at the top of the details shows the reviewer (**any admin** if nobody is named), how long it has waited and how often it was sent back.
- On a task that's **Ready for review**, the reviewer (any admin who didn't do the work) sees **Approve** (it becomes Done) and **Send back**, which asks *What needs another look?* (a short note is required) and moves the task back to **In progress** with your note as a comment. The person who did the work sees **Take back** instead, and can't change the assignee until it's out of review.
- Anyone who can edit a task can tick **Needs review**. Only an admin can turn it off.
- All the work waiting for review is on **Review** in the Slate sidebar. See [How does task review work?](#/help/task-review).

### The fields at the top

Right under the title, six fields sit together in one block, each with its name on the left. When one is empty it says so in faint text, so you can see what's missing at a glance.

- **Assignee**: who does it. It shows on their Slate. Empty shows **Unassigned**, with an **Assignee** dropdown to pick yourself or a teammate. If they can't see the task's client, you'll see *They can't see this client yet, so they won't see this task.* Only the task's owner or an admin can change it, and not while the task is in review.
- **Due**, with an optional time. Empty shows **No due date**. The date saves once you've typed all of it, with the same rules as the due date on a task row.
- **Client** or **Project**: the list the task belongs to (click to open it). Empty shows **No client** or **No project**.
- **Priority**: **High**, **Normal** or **Low**, with the same colored dot as on the task rows.
- **Status**: click it to change it (see below).

On a narrow window or a phone, each value drops under its name.

### More fields

- **Start**: the day work can begin. It can't be after the due date.
- **Section**: where it sits on the client's or project's list, such as Weekly or Monthly.
- **Type**: **Task** or **Reminder**. **Remind me** sets a date and time to nudge you.
- **Repeat**: **Doesn't repeat**, **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Month end**. It needs a due date. Finishing a repeating task creates the next one.
- **Sharing** (only the task's owner, the person who made it, can change it): **Share with the client's team** lets the other staff on that client see and work on it. On a project task it reads **Share with project members**. Unshared tasks are private: only you, the assignee, followers and admins see them.
- **SOP section** (client tasks): link the task to a part of the client's SOP. **View SOP section** shows it right there, and **Open full SOP** opens the client's SOP tab.
- **Description**: details, steps or links.

The people line under the title shows who made the task and who has it. Only the task's owner, its assignee, people on a shared task's team and admins can change it. Everyone else sees it read-only.

### Subtasks and checklist

- **Subtasks** are small tasks of their own, each with its own status and assignee. Click **+ Add subtask**.
- **Checklist** steps are simple ticks inside this task. Saved checklists work the same as before: **Save as my checklist**, then **Use a saved checklist…** on another task.

### Comments and @mentions

Type in **Add a comment. Type @ to mention someone.** and click **Post** (or press **Ctrl+Enter** / **⌘+Enter**). Type **@** and pick a teammate to bring them in: they get a **mentioned you** item in their bell (no email for now) and start following the task. You can edit or delete your own comments. Admins can edit any comment; when someone other than the author changes one, the comment shows who edited it, for example **(edited by** and their name**)**, instead of just **(edited)**.

The tabs above the comments show **All**, just **Comments**, or the **Activity**: a history of every change to the task, who made it and when.

### Client requests

On a client task, **Client requests** lists what you've asked the client for and their answers. **Ask client** sends a question, a document request or an approval to the client's portal users or contacts, and puts the task in **Waiting on client**. Each request has **Email again**, **Send reminder**, **Record answer**, **Accept**, **Cancel** and more. Client emails for requests are a dry run for now: nothing is sent. See [How do I ask a client something from a task?](#/help/ask-client).

### Files and links

- On a **client** task, **Add file** saves the file in that client's Google Drive folder as an internal file (clients don't see it). If the client's Drive isn't connected, you'll see *File storage isn't connected yet. Add a link instead.*
- On an **internal** task (no client), files go to private staff-only storage.
- **Add link** works on any task.
- **Remove** takes a file off the task. A Drive file stays in the client's Drive.

### Followers

Followers see a task's comments and changes, even on a private task. The owner, the assignee and anyone mentioned follow it automatically. At the bottom of the details, click **Follow** (it then says **Following ✓**) or use **Add follower**.

### Delete and restore

**More actions** → **Delete** asks *Delete this task?*: "It leaves every list. Its comments and checklist are kept, and the owner or an admin can restore it." Deleting hides a task; nothing is erased, and that holds for deletes from older screens too.

Right after a delete, the details show **This task was deleted.** with a **Restore** button. Opening a deleted task's link later (from **Copy link** or a bell item) shows the same banner. A deleted task is read-only: you can't comment on it or follow it until it's restored.

### Troubleshooting

- **"Only the task's owner or an admin can change that."** Your change was undone because you can't edit that task or field.
- **"Someone else just changed this task. Showing the latest."** Two people edited at once. Check the task and make your change again if you still need it.
- **"You're offline."** You can read what was loaded. Changes wait until you're back online.
