If something in the app looks broken, or you have an idea or a question, send it from inside the app. It goes straight to the admins.

### Sending a report

1. Click **?** in the top bar, then **Send feedback**. You can also press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K**) and type "feedback", or scroll to the bottom of the **Staff guide** page and click **Report a bug / feedback**.
2. Pick what it is: **Bug** (picked for you), **Idea**, **Question** or **Other**.
3. Describe it. For a bug, say what you were doing and what happened. There's also an optional box, **What did you expect to happen?**
4. Add a screenshot if it helps (it usually does for a bug). See **Adding screenshots** below.
5. Click **Send**. You'll see **Thanks — feedback sent**.

We include the page you were on and the client you had open, so you don't have to explain where you were. For bugs we also include your browser and screen size, which helps us reproduce the problem.

### Adding screenshots

You can attach up to 3 images to any report. There are three ways:

- Click **Add screenshot** and pick an image file.
- Drag an image file onto the window.
- Paste one: press **⌘⇧4** (Mac) or **Win+Shift+S** (Windows) to grab part of the screen, then press **⌘V** / **Ctrl+V** while the window is open.

Each image shows as a small preview. Click **×** on a preview to remove it. Images must be PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF and 10 MB or smaller; anything else is turned away with a message in red.

The images upload when you click **Send** (the button says **Uploading…** meanwhile). If something goes wrong you'll see a red message and your text stays in the boxes, so just click **Send** again. Only you and the admins can see your screenshots.

### Checking on what you sent

Open the same window and click **My feedback** at the bottom. Each report shows its status:

- **New**: not looked at yet
- **Planned**: we're going to fix or build it
- **Done**: fixed or built
- **Won't do**: we decided not to, usually with a note saying why

If an admin replied, the note shows under your report as **Reply:**. Screenshots you attached show as small previews; click one to open it full size in a new tab. You only see your own reports.

### Tips

- One problem per report makes things easier to track.
- Include the client name or the exact button if it matters. Don't paste passwords or bank details, and crop or skip screenshots that show them.
- On a phone, the **?** button is hidden. Search for the **Staff guide** page and use the link at the bottom.
- You can't edit or delete a report after sending. Send another one with the extra details.
