You don't log hours in the portal. There are two separate things, and only one of them is your timesheet.

### 1. Your real hours: QuickBooks Time

- Keep tracking your hours the way you already do, in **QuickBooks Time** (Workforce), against the right customer.
- The portal reads those hours from QuickBooks automatically. Admins use them for the Team page, profitability and capacity.
- The old **My Time** page in the portal was removed. If you have a bookmark to it, it now opens Home.

### 2. In-app time: automatic, not billed

The portal also notes, on its own, roughly how long you spend on each client's pages. It's shown as **In app** time. You don't need to start or stop anything.

It only counts while all of these are true:

- you're signed in as yourself (not a client, and not using "View as"),
- you have one of the client's pages open,
- the browser tab is visible, and
- you've moused, typed, scrolled or tapped in the last 2 minutes.

It's saved about once a minute and when you switch away from the tab. There are daily caps (12 hours per client, 16 hours in total), so a tab left open overnight doesn't pile up time.

> In-app time is a rough guide to where attention goes. It is **not** billed time and doesn't replace QuickBooks Time.

### Troubleshooting

- **My hours look wrong on the Team page.** Check the entries in QuickBooks Time first: the portal only shows what QuickBooks has. The customer on each entry also needs to be matched to a portal client by an admin.
- **In-app time is lower than I expected.** Time stops counting after 2 minutes without any input, and while the tab is hidden. That's by design.
