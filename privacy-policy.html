Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

MyGoodBooks LLC ("MyGoodBooks," "we," "us," or "our") provides a bookkeeping client portal ("the Service") that lets our bookkeeping clients and their authorized users view and build financial reports, message their bookkeeper, share documents, and respond to requests from their bookkeeper. MyGoodBooks staff also use the Service to manage client work and internal team operations. This policy explains what information we collect through the Service, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Account information. Name and email address for client users and MyGoodBooks staff who sign in to the Service, along with the access level assigned to each user.
  • Financial data. When a client organization connects its QuickBooks Online account, we store an encrypted authorization token and the status of the connection, and we retrieve and store financial data from QuickBooks via Intuit's API, including accounts, transactions, reports, budgets, classes and locations, and customer and payer names (for example, donor names and giving totals used for giving statements). We use this data solely to provide the Service. We do not access QuickBooks data unless a client organization has explicitly connected its account.
  • Documents and files. Files that clients or staff upload to the Service, including files provided in response to a bookkeeper's request. Uploaded files are stored in MyGoodBooks' Google Workspace account or our database provider's file storage, and are not shared publicly. Clients can choose which people at their organization can see a file they upload. We also store links and file names for documents clients choose to reference from their own storage.
  • Messages and requests. Messages between clients and their bookkeeper, messages between staff members, and requests for information or documents along with the responses to them.
  • Client contacts and request links. Our staff may record contacts at a client organization who can answer requests (name, email address, and role, such as treasurer or pastor), including people who do not have a sign-in for the Service. When we email a request, the email may include a private link that lets the recipient view and answer that one request and upload files to it without signing in. A link expires after a set period (14 days by default, never more than 30) or once it is answered or replaced. We store only a scrambled (hashed) form of each link, and, to protect the links from abuse, a hashed form of the network (IP) address used to open them. Recipients can stop reminder emails from the link.
  • Organization profile. Information a client organization provides about itself, such as the name to use on cards and gifts, organization type, founding date, fiscal year end, a main contact's name, email, and phone, and a gift mailing address with a delivery contact name and phone, plus the date the organization became our client. An optional "get to know you" section asks about things like favorite treats, coffee or tea, hobbies, and any allergies we should know about. We use this information to keep your records current and to send cards and anniversary gifts. "Get to know you" answers are visible only to MyGoodBooks administrators and the staff assigned to your organization.
  • Reports and shared links. Reports you create in the Service. If you create a shareable link to a report, a snapshot of that report can be viewed by anyone with the link until it expires or you turn it off.
  • Email information. Delivery and engagement status of emails the Service sends (such as notifications and digests), and your email preferences.
  • Usage data. Which pages and features of the Service are used and when, tied to the signed-in account, along with the browser type and app version in use and error reports when something fails, plus optional feedback (including screenshots) you choose to submit.

Information about MyGoodBooks staff

For MyGoodBooks employees and contractors who use the Service, we also process work-related information, including task and project activity, time actively spent in the Service on each client, time off, quarterly performance self-reviews and manager reviews, team survey responses, electronic signatures on reviews and on employee handbook policies, birthdays (month and day only) and work anniversaries, employee records (such as legal name, job title, employment type, hire and end dates, work location, personal contact details, and emergency contacts), and records of company equipment. Signed review documents are stored in MyGoodBooks' Google Drive. This information is used only for internal operations and personnel management, is visible only to the staff member and authorized MyGoodBooks administrators (except that time off may be shown to coworkers who cover the staff member's work), and is never shared with clients. Staff profile photos are stored in the Service and shown to other MyGoodBooks staff.

How we use information

  • To operate and provide the Service, including displaying financial reports, dashboards, and forecasts.
  • To authenticate users and enforce access permissions.
  • To deliver our bookkeeping services and manage client work.
  • To send notifications, digests, and other communications about your account or the Service.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the Service and improve its features.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use client financial data for advertising.

How we share information

We share information only as follows:

  • With Intuit / QuickBooks Online, to authenticate the connection your organization initiates and retrieve the financial data described above.
  • With service providers who operate parts of the Service on our behalf, under obligations to protect your data: Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage), Vercel (hosting), Resend (email delivery), and Google Workspace (file storage).
  • With people you choose, such as recipients of a shareable report link you create.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of MyGoodBooks, our clients, or others.

We do not share your data with any other third party without your consent.

Cookies and local storage

The Service uses browser storage to keep you signed in and remember your settings. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Data location

Data is stored and processed in the United States.

Data retention

We retain account, financial, and document data for as long as your organization is a MyGoodBooks client, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for accounting, legal, or backup purposes. Staff information is retained for the duration of employment and afterward as required for legal and personnel recordkeeping. You may request deletion of your information by contacting us below.

Disconnecting QuickBooks

You may disconnect QuickBooks Online at any time from your Intuit account settings, or by asking us to disconnect it. When a connection is disconnected, we delete the stored authorization token and stop retrieving new data. Financial data already retrieved is kept under the retention terms above so your reports and records stay available; you may request that we delete it by contacting us below.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections, encrypted storage of authorization tokens, access controls, and row-level security on our database. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a security incident affecting your information, we will notify affected clients without unreasonable delay and as required by law.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may manage email notifications from your settings or the unsubscribe link in our emails. Contact us at the email below with any request.

Children's privacy

The Service is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date, and we will notify users by email before material changes take effect.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to admin@mygoodbooks.org.
