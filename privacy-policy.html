Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

MyGoodBooks LLC ("MyGoodBooks," "we," "us," or "our") provides a bookkeeping client portal ("the Service") that lets our bookkeeping clients and their staff view financial reports, message their bookkeeper, and manage document and integration access for their organization. This policy explains what information we collect through the Service, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Account information. Email address and name, for staff and client users signing in to the portal.
  • Financial data. When a client connects their QuickBooks Online account, we store an encrypted authorization token for that account and the status of the connection. As we roll out reporting features, we will access transactions, accounts, and budget data from QuickBooks via Intuit's API solely to display them in the Service. We do not access QuickBooks data unless a client organization has explicitly connected their account.
  • Document references. Client-provided links to documents stored in their own Google Drive. We store the link and a file name only — we do not upload, host, or store the underlying document.
  • Messages. Messages sent within the Service between staff members, and, once client messaging launches, between clients and their bookkeeper.
  • Usage data. Which pages of the Service are viewed and when, tied to the signed-in account, plus optional feedback you choose to submit.

How we use information

  • To operate and provide the Service, including displaying financial reports and dashboards.
  • To authenticate users and enforce access permissions set by MyGoodBooks staff.
  • To communicate with you about your account or the Service.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the Service.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use client financial data for advertising.

How we share information

We share information only as follows:

  • With Intuit / QuickBooks Online, to authenticate the connection your organization initiates and retrieve the financial data described above.
  • With service providers who host our infrastructure (Supabase for our database and authentication, Vercel for hosting), under obligations to protect your data.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of MyGoodBooks, our clients, or others.

We do not share your data with any other third party without your consent.

Data retention

We retain account and financial data for as long as your organization is a MyGoodBooks client, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for accounting, legal, or backup purposes. You may request deletion of your account information by contacting us below.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections, access controls, and row-level security on our database. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may disconnect your QuickBooks Online connection at any time from your Intuit account settings. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the email below.

Children's privacy

The Service is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to jeff@mygoodbooks.org.
